Getting Started

Start a Project

Open an image – On the start screen, choose Open Image or drop a PNG, JPG, or GIF onto the workspace. The image becomes the Base Image, the foundation of the canvas.

  • Images larger than 1024 × 1024px are resized to fit. Glitter shows the new dimensions first, and you can continue or cancel. Your original file is never changed.
  • The maximum file size is 10MB.
  • Once a document is open, dropping another image onto the workspace adds it as a new Sticker layer. It does not replace the canvas.

Start blank – Click a size on the start screen to get a white canvas. For other sizes, click More Presets and choose from Social Media, Web Classics, or General, which includes the classic 400 × 400 Blingee size. You can also enter custom dimensions and choose a color or a transparent background.

Open a project – Click Open Project on the start screen, or drag a .json project file onto the workspace.

Before it replaces the current project, Glitter checks the file and reports:

  • Stickers or fonts that are unavailable, and unknown layer types.
  • Files created by a newer version of the editor.

Review any substitutions or skipped content, then choose Open Anyway. Do not save over the original of a file from a newer version. Data this version does not support is lost when you save.

Basics

Tools

Each tool can show its own options in the context bar. Some options appear only when a certain layer is selected. Every tool has a shortcut key, listed in Keyboard Shortcuts & Gestures.

{ui-tool:select} – Press V. Pick layers and move stickers, text, shapes, and paths. Turn off Auto-Select in the options to keep moving the layer selected in the Layers panel when you drag across other artwork.

  • See Transform for moving, resizing, and rotating.
  • See Selection & Alignment for selecting several layers and aligning them.

{ui-tool:hand} – Press H, then drag to pan. Hold Space to use it temporarily from any tool. Double-click the Hand tool to fit the canvas.

  • Touch – Pan with one or two fingers from any tool. The Hand tool is hidden on touch screens.
  • Mouse – Middle-button drag pans from any tool.
  • Trackpad – Swipe with two fingers to pan in any direction.
  • Context bar – Center the canvas horizontally or vertically.

{ui-tool:zoom} – Press Z. Click to zoom in, Alt-click to zoom out, or drag right/up and left/down to scrub the zoom. Double-click the Zoom tool to return to 100%.

  • Touch – Pinch with two fingers.
  • Trackpad – Pinch to zoom around the gesture position.

Context bar options:

  • Fit – Fit the whole image on screen.
  • Fill – Fill the screen with the image.
  • Zoom to Selection – Press Shift2 to center and fit the selected layer or group.
  • Reset – Double-click the zoom percentage in the status bar to return to 100%.

{ui-tool:crop} – Press C. Edit the canvas bounds. See Canvas Size & Crop.

{ui-tool:glitterFill} – Press I. With a glitter layer or the Base Image selected, click the canvas to select a color from the Base Image. See Select Colors.

{ui-tool:brush} – Press B. Paint or erase the mask of a Glitter Fill layer on the canvas. See Glitter Brush.

{ui-tool:text} – Press T. Click the canvas to place Point text, or drag to draw a Box that wraps text inside a resizable frame. See Text.

{ui-tool:shape} – Press U. Drag to draw a shape at that size (hold Shift for a square), or click for the default size. See Shapes.

{ui-tool:line} – Press L. Drag to draw a straight line (hold Shift for 45° steps), or click for the default length. See Draw a Line or an Arrow.

{ui-tool:pen} – Press P. Click to place the points of a path, drag to pull a curve, and click the first point to close the path. On a selected path, the Pen edits its points. See Draw with the Pen and Edit Points.

The Library

The Library window opens beside the Inspector on desktop and as a sheet on phones. It holds everything you can pick: glitter, stickers, shapes, brush tips, and fonts. It shows one kind at a time and switches to match what you are doing. For example, changing a shape opens Shapes, and choosing a glitter for its fill opens Glitter. Its header names what your pick will change. Press Close when you have what you want.

Recent and Favorites – Your last picks of each kind appear in a Recent row at the top. Hover a card and click its heart to add it to Favorites, which appears first. Both are stored in this browser and survive Reset Everything.

Search and filter – Every kind has a search box and filters. Glitter and stickers open in category folders. Shapes and fonts list everything under category headings.

Working with Layers

The Layers panel lists every layer. Layers at the top appear in front.

Add – Click + and choose a layer type: Glitter Fill, Sticker, Text, Shape, Line, Frame, Sparkles, or Filter.

Reorder – Drag a layer up or down. On touch screens, drag by its handle.

Show or hide – Click the eye icon.

Lock – Click the lock icon to block canvas transforms and property changes. A locked layer can still be selected and inspected. The Base Image stays editable, because its lock means it is fixed to the canvas, not read-only.

Duplicate – Click the duplicate icon to copy a layer with all its settings. You can also hold Alt while dragging on the canvas, or press Ctrl/CmdD.

Delete – Click the trash icon.

Rename – Double-click a layer name. Double-click a text layer on the canvas to edit its text.

Blend – In Appearance, use Blend (below Opacity) to set how a Glitter Fill, Sticker, Text, or Shape layer combines with everything below it. The stack is composited from bottom to top. Filter layers use the blend operations built into their effects.

Transform

Select a layer to show its bounding box on the canvas.

Move, resize, and rotate – Drag inside the box to move. Drag a corner handle to resize or scale. Drag along an edge to resize one axis. Drag just outside a corner to rotate.

  • Shift while resizing – Temporarily inverts the aspect lock.
  • Alt while resizing – Resizes around the anchor.
  • Ctrl/Cmd while resizing – Bypasses snapping.
  • Shift while rotating (with the handle or the Rotation slider) – Snaps to 15° steps.
  • Shift while moving – Constrains movement to one axis.
  • Text – Point text scales with the handles. Box text resizes its frame without changing the type size.

Lock Aspect – When on, the corner handles and the circular middle-edge handles keep the layer's aspect ratio. Hold Shift to flip the lock for one drag. If that drag changes the proportions while the lock was on, the lock turns off.

Anchor – The crosshair in the middle of a selected layer. It is the point the layer rotates, flips, and animates around, and the point that X and Y report.

  • Drag it to move it. It snaps to the layer's corners, edge midpoints, and center, and to the canvas center.
  • Hold Shift to jump to the nearest corner, edge, or center. Hold Ctrl/Cmd to place it freely.
  • Alt-click inside the layer to put the anchor there. Double-click the anchor to reset it to the center.
  • You can also pick a position from Anchor, under Advanced in Transform.

Transform card – Enter exact X/Y position, W/H size, rotation, and opacity. It also has alignment, flip, and reset controls.

  • Nudge – Arrow keys move the selected layer 1px. Hold Shift for 10px.
  • Field stepping – In the X/Y/W/H fields, ↑/↓ step 1px. Hold Shift for 10px.

Multiple layers – When several movable layers are selected, a shared bounding box appears. Drag inside it to move the group, or use the corner handles to scale it uniformly. See Selection & Alignment for how to build a selection.

  • Touch – A tap selects the topmost layer. Add or remove layers in the Layers panel, then move or pinch the shared box on the canvas.

Selection & Alignment

Select several layers to move, align, or edit them together.

Select layers

  • Marquee – Drag on empty canvas to select the layers it touches. Hold Shift to add to the current selection.
  • On the canvas – Shift-click toggles the layer under the pointer. Alt-click cycles through overlapping layers.
  • In the Layers panel – ShiftClick selects a range. Ctrl/CmdClick toggles a single layer, including locked, Base Image, and Glitter Fill layers.
  • Everything – Ctrl/CmdA selects every movable layer. Escape clears the selection.

A selection that includes a protected or non-transformable layer (locked, pinned, the Base Image, or an empty Glitter Fill) can still be selected, but it cannot move as a group.

Align and distribute – Choose Selection or Canvas as the reference, then:

  • Align left, center, or right, and top, middle, or bottom.
  • Distribute three or more layers horizontally or vertically.
  • With one sticker, text, shape, or path layer selected, center it horizontally or vertically on the canvas.
  • Duplicate and Delete are under Actions.

Smart Guides and snapping – While you move a sticker, text, shape, or multi-layer selection, its edges and center snap to the canvas and to visible layers. Pink guide lines show the active alignment.

  • Snapping is on by default. Use Snapping below the preview to turn it on or off.
  • Hold Ctrl during a move to disable snapping temporarily.
  • Smart Guides apply to moving only. Resizing and rotation are freeform, except for Shift rotation snapping and Ctrl/Cmd sticker resizing in whole steps.

Commands & Shortcuts – Open it from the header to browse every shortcut and gesture. Use the Keyboard and Canvas Gestures tabs, or search by command, key, device, gesture, or section. Key labels match your system (Mac or Windows). Searches treat Command/Control and Option/Alt as the same key. The full list is also in Keyboard Shortcuts & Gestures.

  • Undo – Ctrl/CmdZ.
  • Redo – Ctrl/CmdY or Ctrl/CmdShiftZ.
  • Copy and paste – Ctrl/CmdC copies the selected layers. Ctrl/CmdV pastes copied layers or an image from the clipboard.
  • Escape – Closes an open picker or cancels a transform in progress. Press it again to clear the selection.

Canvas Size & Crop

The canvas size is the size of the exported image. Change it with the {ui-tool:crop} tool or in the Base Image settings. Both change the canvas bounds without scaling your layers, except the Image option, which scales everything.

{ui-tool:crop} – Press C to edit the canvas bounds. The bounds can extend beyond the current canvas.

  • Resize – Drag a handle.
  • Move – Drag inside the bounds. Edges and center snap.
  • Draw new bounds – Drag outside the current bounds.
  • Size panel – Shows the same rectangle and adds ratios, named sizes, fit to artwork or selection, padding, and extension fill. Swap changes the orientation.
  • Apply – Press Enter or double-click inside the bounds. Applying is one undo step.
  • Cancel – Press Escape.

Crop modifiers

  • A locked ratio wins. Shift toggles it for one drag.
  • Alt resizes from the center.
  • Ctrl bypasses snapping.
  • Arrow keys move the bounds 1px. Hold Shift for 10px.
  • Touch – Use one finger to edit and two to pan or zoom. Use Done and Cancel in the context bar.

Base Image settings – Select the protected Base Image layer to open {ui-panel:baseLayerSettingsSection}.

  • Background – Choose Image, Transparent, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Replace swaps the base image without disturbing other layers.
  • Image – Enter a linked pixel size or percentage to resize the canvas and every editable layer together. Choose whether glitter textures and visual effects scale with the design.
  • Canvas – Crop or extend the canvas without scaling content. Use Relative for pixel deltas, choose an anchor, and fill the new area with transparency or a color.
  • Fit – Crop or extend the canvas to fit all artwork or the selected layers, with optional padding on every side. In Canvas mode you can also choose a ratio or a named size.

Canvas View Options

These controls change what you see on the canvas. They do not change the export.

Toggle Bounds – Outline the canvas boundary and clip artwork that extends beyond it, so the preview shows what the export contains.

Toggle Transparency – Show or hide the checkerboard behind transparent areas.

Show Only Selected Layer – Isolate the selected layer on the canvas. Click again to show every visible layer. This does not change layer visibility settings.

Pause motion – Freeze layer motion, sparkles, animated stickers, and animated fills and effects at their first frame so you can edit without distraction. This only pauses the canvas preview; exports keep their animation.

Mobile

On phones and tablets, the start screen fills the workspace. Open an image, create a preset canvas, or open a project. After that, a scrolling property bar and sheets give you the same controls as the desktop Inspector.

Property bar – Swipe sideways to see every section. Tap a chip to open its sheet; tap another to switch sections.

  • Layers – Stacking order and selection. Drag layers by their handles. The plus opens the full Add menu.
  • Properties – The selected layer's sections. More holds actions. With nothing selected, plus-marked Sticker, Fill Layer, Shape and Text buttons add a new layer.
  • Library – Open it from an asset or paint row. Back returns to the section you came from; Close dismisses the sheet.

Property sheet – Drag the sheet's handle to resize it. Swipe down, tap the open chip, or press Close to dismiss it. Opening an effect leaves it off until you use its switch. Drawing tools pause while their rail is hidden by the sheet.

Gestures – On touch, pan with one or two fingers from any tool and pinch to zoom. A tap selects the topmost layer. On a trackpad, swipe with two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom.

App Settings – Tap the gear button in the header.

About and Guide – Use the navigation bar to search the document, open its Contents, or return to the top. On phones, both open as near-full-screen sheets.

Glitter

Create a Glitter Layer

A Glitter Fill layer fills parts of the Base Image with glitter. It is empty until you select colors or paint a mask.

In the Layers panel, click + and choose Glitter Fill.

Choose a Glitter

Browse the glitter gallery and click a glitter to apply it to the selected colors. Use search and filters to find a style, color, or category.

Select Colors

The glitter layer fills only the colors you select from the Base Image.

Use the Glitter Fill tool (I) and click a color in the Base Image.

  • The glitter layer stays empty until you make a selection.
  • The selected area is outlined in blue in the preview. The outline is not exported. It is desktop only, because it is too resource-intensive on mobile.

Selection Options

Multi-Select – Make several selections on the same layer.

Threshold – Sets how closely a color must match the one you clicked.

  • Lower – Selects only colors very close to the one clicked.
  • Higher – Selects a broader range of similar colors across the whole image.

Feather – Softens the edges of the selection.

Contiguous – Selects only connected areas of the matching color, so scattered pixels elsewhere are left out.

Invert – Flips the finished mask, so glitter covers everything except the selected and painted areas.

Adjust {ui-panel:glitterSettingsSection}

Select a glitter layer to open {ui-panel:glitterSettingsSection}. Texture Scale (under Advanced → Texture), Opacity, Threshold, and Feather are set separately for each glitter fill layer.

Change – In the Fill asset card, click Change to open the glitter Library for this layer. Its header names the destination. Choose a glitter, then press Close.

Opacity – Sets transparency. 100% is fully visible and 0% is invisible.

Source – Each fill slot can use Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. These slots also appear on Sticker, Text, Shape, and Canvas Background layers, and they match in exports.

Gradient editor

  • Click the bar to add a stop.
  • Drag a stop to move it. Hold Shift to snap to 5% steps. Hold Alt while dragging to duplicate it. Stops also catch at 0, 25, 50, 75, and 100%.
  • Focus a stop and press the arrow keys to move it 1%, or Shift + arrow keys to move it 5%. Press Delete or Backspace to remove it.

Advanced – Expand it for Scale (glitter pattern size), Hue, Saturation, and Brightness, plus texture anchoring and offsets.

Glitter Brush

Use the Glitter Brush to paint or erase exactly where a Glitter Fill layer appears, in addition to color selections. Text and Shape layers use their own rendered outlines and cannot hold painted masks.

Start painting – Click the Glitter Brush in the toolbar, or press B. With a Glitter Fill layer selected, strokes edit that layer. Otherwise the first stroke creates a new Glitter Fill layer. Erasing needs an existing Glitter Fill layer.

Paint / Erase – Switch to Erase in the context bar below the canvas to remove glitter instead of adding it. This includes areas added by color selection. Press E for Erase, or X to swap between Paint and Erase. On a pen tablet, the pen's eraser end erases for that stroke.

Brush settings – Pick the tip (round, square, calligraphy, star, or heart), then set:

  • Size – The brush diameter. Press [ and ] to change it, or hold Shift for larger steps.
  • Softness – Edge feathering.
  • Flow – How much glitter builds up per stroke.
  • Spacing – How far apart the stamps sit along the stroke.
  • Smoothing – How much shaky strokes are stabilized.

Paint and Erase keep separate settings, and both are remembered between sessions.

Settings groups – Brush Settings and Eraser Settings are organized into Content (the brush tip), Stroke, and Actions. Pressure Sensitivity is in Stroke, with the dynamics it affects. Actions can reset the active tool, copy the other tool's settings, or Clear Paint.

Straight lines – Hold Shift while dragging to constrain the stroke to a straight line at 0°, 45°, or 90°.

Invert and Clear Paint – Invert is described under Selection Options. Clear Paint removes all painted strokes and keeps color selections.

Mask Overlay – Toggle it in the preview controls to highlight painted areas while you work.

Move a painted fill – A painted or color-picked fill is a layer like any other. With the Select tool, drag it to move it, or use its handles and the Transform card to resize, rotate, and flip it. The fill, its outline, shadow, and sparkles move together, and later brush strokes still land under the pointer.

The painted area stays the size of the canvas, so after a move, part of the canvas lies outside the layer. To paint there, use a new Glitter Fill layer. To put the layer back where it was painted, use Reset Transform.

Auto Glitter Beta

Auto Glitter scans the Base Image and turns its colors into animated Glitter Fill layers. It is available when Canvas Background is set to Image. It opens the Auto Glitter panel and previews the glitter live on the canvas. It is in Beta and still being refined.

Temporary mode – While Auto Glitter is active, an Auto Glitter Mode strip appears above the navigation controls. It shows status only while analyzing, updating, or reporting an error. Choose Exit to discard the session.

  • Navigation – The tool switches to Hand. Hand, Zoom, the mouse wheel, Space-drag, and touch pan and pinch all work.
  • Select a match – Switch to Select, then click visible glitter on the canvas to open that Color Match in the Library.
  • Editing is locked – Layer selection, undo and redo, and the editing tools stay locked until you create or discard the preview.

Compare – Use Original / Flat / Glitter at the top of the panel to compare the source image, the flattened posterized colors, and the final glitter. The create and cancel actions stay at the bottom. Palette, Color Matches, and Advanced scroll between them.

Palette – Colors and Combine Similar update the preview instantly, without repeating the image scan. Style sets which colors are kept and how strongly the matched glitter is saturated:

  • Faithful – The default. Stays closest to the image's colors and does not boost saturation.
  • Balanced – Favors colorful regions slightly and adds a small saturation boost.
  • Vibrant – Prioritizes colorful accents, combines more neutral shades, and boosts saturation strongly.

Black-and-white source colors stay monochrome. Auto Glitter removes color casts from their matched glitter.

Color Matches – Each detected color appears as a match. Select a match swatch to open the Library on that match. Choosing a glitter updates that one match in the preview. Back returns to Auto Glitter on a phone; Close dismisses the Library.

  • Skip a color – Click the match's eye button.
  • Combine colors – Drag one color row onto another. On touch screens, drag by the row handle.

Advanced – Set the minimum region detail, clean up anti-aliased edges and dither noise, or turn off hue tuning of matched glitter.

Create – Create N Layers turns the preview into normal, editable Glitter Fill layers. Transparent areas of the image stay transparent, the Base Image is unchanged, and your previous tool is restored.

  • Existing set – If an Auto Glitter set exists, choose Edit to reopen its regions and swatches, Replace to build a new set in its place, or Add to keep it and create another. If you have edited the generated layers, the default is Add, which is safer.
  • Apply Changes – When editing a set, this replaces the set in place. Changing Palette or Advanced while editing rebuilds the set from the Base Image.

Discard – Cancel, Exit, Escape, replacing the Base Image, or leaving the panel discards the preview. Before the layers are created, Glitter asks you to confirm.

Stickers

Add a Sticker

A Sticker layer places a decorative image on top of the layers below it.

Add the layer – Click + in the Layers panel and choose Sticker. The layer is empty until you choose a sticker.

Pick a sticker – Browse the gallery and click a sticker to add it.

Position it – Use the Select tool (V) to drag the sticker.

{ui-panel:stickerSettingsSection}

Change – In the Asset card, click Change to swap in a different sticker without recreating the layer.

Advanced – Expand it to adjust Hue, Saturation, and Brightness. Each control resets independently.

Transform – Enter exact position, size, rotation, and opacity, or use the alignment, flip, and reset controls. See Transform.

Handles – Drag a corner handle to scale proportionally, or a side handle to resize one axis. Stickers always land on whole pixels. Hold Ctrl/Cmd while resizing to step through 100%, 200%, 300%, and so on. Pixel art keeps every pixel the same size at these steps.

Lock Aspect – The lock at the end of the Size row. It keeps width and height proportional while you resize. Turn it off to stretch or squash the sticker.

Flip Horizontally / Vertically – Mirrors the sticker.

Outline – Traces the sticker's shape in glitter or a solid color, with smooth or pixel edges. Fill inside extends the outline paint behind the whole sticker as a backing. Use all animation frames keeps the outline of an animated sticker steady.

Shadow – An optional paint slot with the same sources as text and shapes: glitter (with its own swatch and Advanced Hue, Saturation, and Brightness), solid color, or gradient.

Advanced – Fit canvas scales and centers the sticker so it fits fully inside the canvas. Fill canvas scales it to cover the canvas completely.

Stretchable Stickers & Frames

Assets marked Stretchable keep their corners when you resize one axis. They use nine-slice geometry: four independent insets protect the corners, and the edges and center stretch or repeat. A banner can protect only its left and right caps. Use the Stretchable library filter to find these assets.

Smart Stretch – On by default when you choose a stretchable sticker. Turn it off in Transform for ordinary image stretching. While you resize, dashed guides show the protected slices. Animated assets keep their animation.

Remove Backgrounds & Uploads

Remove background – Turns an uploaded PNG or JPG sticker into a cutout. Select the sticker, then use the action in Sticker Properties or the Select bar.

  • The first use downloads the remover. Processing stays on your device. Cancel stops it.
  • Review Before and After, then choose Keep or Discard. Keep preserves the original in My Stickers and can be undone.
  • Finished cutouts, animated GIFs, and the Base Image are not eligible. To try again, start from the original upload.

Delete an upload – Open My Stickers or My Glitter, click the trash button on the tile, and confirm Delete. On a computer, the button appears when you hover or focus the tile. On a phone, it is always visible.

  • This removes the asset from the Library and Favorites.
  • Layers that already use it keep working. Undo cannot restore the Library item.

Text

Create a Text Layer

Add the layer – Click + in the Layers panel and choose Text, or press T and click or drag on the canvas.

  • Click – Places Point text, which fits tightly around the glyphs.
  • Drag – Draws a Box, which wraps text inside a resizable frame.

Fill with glitter – Browse the glitter gallery and click a glitter to fill the text with it, as on a glitter layer.

Typography & Layout

Select a text layer to open {ui-panel:textSettingsSection}. There you edit the copy, choose a non-destructive letter case, and set the layout.

Editing – New text starts as selected placeholder copy, so typing replaces it. The Text tool edits text and the Select tool moves it.

  • With the Text tool, click text to place the caret, drag to select, or double-click a word.
  • With the Select tool, drag text to move it. Double-click or double-tap it to switch to the Text tool and edit.
  • The panel's text field shows the same copy and shares the caret.
  • Press Enter for a new line. Press Escape or click off the text to commit the edit and return to Select. Empty text is removed when editing ends.
  • Pasting plain text outside a field creates a text layer at the canvas center.

Context bar – While editing, the context bar has font size, Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, and alignment. Justify spreads the gaps on wrapped lines in a box. The last line of a paragraph stays left aligned.

Text – Type on the canvas or in the text field. The symbols under the field insert at the caret. Case includes alternating caps, which skips spaces and symbols. With Color emoji in the Type group, emoji keep their system colors and still get an outline and shadow.

Font – The Font card shows the current font. Click Change to choose another from the Library's Fonts, sorted by style and language. Press Close when you have chosen.

Font Size, Letter Spacing, and Line Height – Set the glyph size, the gap between characters, and the spacing between lines. Line Height works in any text mode, for wrapped lines and explicit line breaks.

Point / Auto Height / Fixed – Sets how the text is framed.

  • Point fits the copy and scales with the corner handles.
  • Auto Height wraps at a fixed width and grows with the text. Side handles change the width. Top and bottom handles convert it to Fixed.
  • Fixed wraps inside the width and height you draw.

Vertical and Horizontal alignment – Position the text inside a Box frame (top, middle, bottom) and align its lines (left, center, right).

Orientation – Choose Stacked to put each character on its own centered row. Enter starts a new column to the right. Line Height sets the spacing between rows and Letter Spacing sets the spacing between columns. The WordArt style gallery includes five stacked looks and the original Office paper and stone textures.

Actions

  • Fit box to text – Fits a Fixed frame to its content.
  • Split text – Replaces the layer with one layer per Character, Word, or Line, at their current positions. Each piece keeps its style and its own background plate. Glitter asks before it drops hidden overflow text, and refuses a split that exceeds the 25-layer limit. Undo restores the original layer.

Fill, Background, Outline & Shadow

Fill, Outline, and Shadow each keep their own source: glitter, solid color, or gradient. Fill can also be None. Background uses its own glitter or solid source.

Fill – Choose None, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Texture Scale is under Advanced → Texture for glitter. Opacity applies to any visible source. Fill set to None with an Outline on gives outlined text.

Background – Enable a shape behind the text and choose a Glitter or Solid source. Start from a preset, or set the horizontal and vertical padding, corner radius, texture, and opacity yourself.

  • Lines – Draws around each line. Keep lines Separate, Merge nearby lines into a stepped shape, or use Connected for a more permissive join. Merge Distance and Spacing Sensitivity control when lines connect.
  • Text Bounds – Draws one shape around all visible text.
  • Text Box – Fills the whole frame of Box text.

Outline – Enable an outline around the text and set its width. Choose Glitter, Solid, or Gradient, then set:

  • Smooth or pixel edges.
  • Outside, on-edge, or inside placement.
  • Whether the outline sits behind or on top of the fill.

Pixel edges grow by four-connected pixel expansion. This leaves out diagonal corner pixels, so outlines follow diagonal contours cleanly.

Shadow – Enable a drop shadow and set its X/Y offset. Like the outline, it has its own glitter, solid color, or gradient.

Choose where glitter goes – Only one swatch (Fill, Background, Outline, or Shadow) is active for glitter picking at a time. Click a swatch to send the next glitter you pick in the gallery to that part of the text.

Shapes

Draw a Shape

Shapes are vector layers that you fill with glitter, a solid color, or a gradient. The Library's Shapes lists every shape under these headings: Basic Shapes, Stars & Bursts, Hearts & Flowers, Nature & Weather, Arrows & Banners, Speech & Music, and Symbols & Signs.

Draw – Click the Shape tool in the toolbar, or press U. Drag on the canvas to draw the shape at that size (hold Shift to keep it square or circular). Click for a default-size shape.

Choose a shape – Pick one from the Library's Shapes and scroll through the category headings. If a shape layer is selected, your pick replaces its shape. Use Add Layer to create a shape before choosing one from the Library.

Edit – {ui-panel:shapeSettingsSection} controls the selected shape's fill, transform, outline, and shadow. Resize, rotate, and move it with the transform handles. Shapes re-render at their new size, so edges stay sharp. Under Transform's Advanced, Fit canvas and Fill canvas center the shape and size it to fit inside the canvas or to cover it.

Fill, Outline & Shadow

Each shape has a Fill, plus an optional Outline and Shadow. Fill can be an image, glitter, a solid color, or a gradient. Outline and Shadow can be glitter, a solid color, or a gradient.

Fill – Choose None, Image, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Fill set to None with an Outline on gives an outline shape.

  • The shape crops image fills.
  • Under Advanced, choose Cover, Contain, Fill, None, or Scale Down. You can also adjust scale and position, tile the image, or switch between smooth and pixelated rendering.

Outline and Shadow – Enable each and pick its own glitter, solid color, or gradient, plus width or offset and opacity. Open Advanced on any slot to recolor its glitter with Hue, Saturation, and Brightness.

  • Outlines follow the shape's true edge and can be solid or dotted.
  • Outlines also have smooth or pixel edges, outside, on-edge, or inside placement, and behind or on-top layering.

Lines & Paths

Draw a Line or an Arrow

A path is a layer made of points joined by straight or curved segments. A line is the simplest path: two points. A path has a Stroke (the line itself) and a Fill (the area inside), each in glitter or a color.

Draw – Click the Line tool in the toolbar, or press L. Drag from the start to the end (hold Shift for horizontal, vertical, and 45° lines). Click for a line of the default length. You can also click + in the Layers panel and choose Line.

Presets – In {ui-panel:pathSettingsSection}, pick a preset to turn the line into an Arrow, a Double Arrow, a Dashed or Dotted line, or a Heart String. Presets change only the dashes and the shapes on the ends. The glitter, color, and width stay as they are.

Select and transform – With the Select tool, a path moves, resizes, rotates, and flips like any other layer. Click directly on the line to pick it. A click beside a diagonal line reaches the layer under it. Resizing moves the points and leaves no stretched pixels. The stroke width follows the resize when Scale Outlines & Effects is on in Settings.

Draw with the Pen

The Pen draws any outline, one point at a time. Trace part of the picture and close the path to get a glitter region with exactly the edge you drew.

Place points – Click the Pen tool, or press P.

  • Click – Places a corner point.
  • Click and drag – Places a smooth point and pulls its handles, which bend the path on both sides.
  • Shift – Keeps a segment or handle on 45° steps.
  • Alt while dragging – Moves one handle without the other.

Next point – This setting in the bar under the canvas chooses what a click places. Corner places corners. Curve places points that the path flows through, with handles calculated from the points on either side, so a few clicks trace a smooth outline. Click the point you just placed to switch it between the two.

Close or finish – Click the first point to close the path. A closed path you just drew switches to a glitter Fill with no Stroke. An open path keeps its Stroke and has no Fill. Press Enter or Esc to finish a path and leave it open.

Undo a point – Every point is its own undo step. Press Ctrl/Cmd + Z or Backspace to remove the last point and keep drawing. Hold Space to pan, or scroll and pinch to zoom, without ending the path. A path left with a single point is removed.

Touch – Tap to place points. Drag from empty canvas to pull a curve. Two fingers always pan and zoom, and never place a point. Use Close and Done in the bar under the canvas.

Edit Points

The Pen also reshapes existing paths. With the Select tool, double-click a path to switch to the Pen with its points showing. You can also select the path and press Enter, or use Edit points in {ui-panel:pathSettingsSection}.

Points – Click a point to select it. Shift-click adds to the selection. Drag across empty canvas to select every point in a box. Drag a point to move it with the rest of the selection, or nudge with the arrow keys (Shift for 10px). Points land on whole pixels and snap to the canvas, to other layers, and into line with the path's other points.

Handles – A selected point shows its handles. The point type sets how they move:

  • Corner – The two handles move independently.
  • Smooth – The handles stay in one line.
  • Mirrored – The handles stay in one line and the same length.

Set the type in the bar under the canvas or in the Point section, which also has the point's exact X and Y. Double-click a point to switch it between a corner and a smooth point.

Segments – Click a segment to add a point there without changing the outline. Drag a segment to bend it. A straight segment becomes a curve. Press Delete to remove the selected points. The path closes up around them and keeps as much of its outline as it can.

Continue, close, finish – Click either end of an open path to keep drawing from it.

  • Close path – Joins the two ends.
  • Reverse – Swaps which end is the start.
  • Swap ends – Trades the shapes on the two ends.
  • Esc clears the point selection, then leaves the editor. Enter or Done leaves it immediately.

From a shape – Convert to path in {ui-panel:shapeSettingsSection} replaces a shape with a path that has the same outline, position, fill, and effects, so you can edit its points.

Fill, Stroke & Outline

Stroke is the line the Pen draws. Outline is an effect around the whole object, as on text and shapes. A line can have an outline of its own.

Stroke – Glitter or a color, with a Width.

  • Edges – Smooth or sharp corners.
  • Cap – How an open line ends: flat, round, or square.
  • Dashes – Turns the line into dashes (with their own Dash and Gap lengths) or dots. Dash offset slides them along the line.

Ends – Put a shape on the Start or End of an open line: arrows, a triangle, a diamond, a circle, a bar, a star, a heart, and more. Each has a Size measured against the line width and turns to follow the direction of the path. It is painted with the stroke, so glitter runs unbroken from the line into the arrowhead.

Effects – Outline, Shadow, Bevel & gloss, and Sparkles work as they do on shapes and follow whatever the path shows. With no Fill, the outline wraps both sides of the line, and the shadow is the line's shadow.

Frames

Add a Frame

A frame is its own layer: a border around the whole picture, painted with glitter, a solid color, or a gradient, or built from an ornate frame image.

Add the layer – Click + in the Layers panel and choose Frame. Pick a preset in {ui-panel:frameSettingsSection}, such as Double Line or Raised, then adjust it. Presets set only the frame's shape. Its glitter, color, or gradient comes from Fill.

Style – Draws a classic web border: solid, double, dotted, dashed, inset, outset, groove, or ridge. The bevel styles shade the frame's own paint lighter and darker, so a glitter frame gets a bevel too. Bevel Depth sets the strength of the shading.

Image – Uses one of the ornate frames from the sticker library, stretched or fitted to the frame.

Stretchable frame images – Smart Stretch keeps the corners around the frame box. Border Scale changes the protected border size from 25 to 400 percent. Stretch and Fit are still available for ordinary image placement. See Stretchable Stickers & Frames.

Fit to Canvas – On by default. The frame follows the canvas edges, even when you resize the canvas, and Inset moves it in from the edge. Turn it off to move, resize, and rotate the frame like a shape, for example to frame one photo in a collage.

Filters

Add a Filter Layer

A filter layer draws nothing of its own. It re-processes everything composited below it, live on the canvas and in the export.

Add the layer – Click + in the Layers panel and choose Filter. It starts as a Basic adjustment. You can switch types at any time in {ui-panel:filterSettingsSection}.

Order – A filter affects only the layers below it. Drag it in the Layers panel to change what it reaches. Stack several filter layers to combine effects. Each one processes the already-filtered result of everything below it. A filter with nothing beneath it still affects the canvas background.

No transform – Filter layers have no position, size, or transform handles. The transform tools and alignment skip them.

Filter Types

Pick a type from the picker at the top of {ui-panel:filterSettingsSection}. Each type has its own controls. Opacity fades the whole filter layer, as on every other layer type.

Instagram – Pick a classic preset from the list. Strength fades the preset back toward the original image. Show filter name adds a centered caption with the preset's name. The caption stays fully visible at Strength 0. Use layer Opacity to fade it.

Basic – Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, and Hue in one adjustment. To fade them independently, stack two Basic filters.

Invert, Grayscale, and Sepia – Each has a single Amount slider.

Tint – Choose a warming, cooling, or color preset, or set a custom color. Density sets the strength of the color cast.

Vignette – Amount, Midpoint, Roundness, Feather, and Color shape the edge. Dark colors darken with Multiply and light colors brighten with Screen, so both black and white work.

Grain – Amount, Size, Roughness, and a Monochrome toggle add film-like texture without enlarging pixels. It stays sharp at any zoom or export size.

Blur – A single Radius slider softens everything beneath the layer.

Pixel Filters

The Pixel & Damage category holds the retro color and mosaic looks. Like every filter, they process everything beneath them. To treat only the Base Image, put one directly above it.

Pixelate – Groups pixels into crisp mosaic cells. Posterize and Dither also have their own Pixel Size. It reduces colors per cell, so dither dots stay one cell wide.

Posterize – Flattens everything below into a set number of solid color regions.

  • Colors – How many colors the palette is reduced to.
  • Style and Combine Similar – How much the palette favors vivid or neutral colors, and how readily near-identical colors merge.
  • Detail and Clean Edges – Tidy small specks and jagged region boundaries.

Dither – Approximates colors with a repeating pattern instead of flat regions. Pick a pattern and a palette, then set Strength to control how pronounced the pattern is. Colors, Style, and Combine Similar appear with the Auto palette. Angle appears with Halftone.

Patterns

  • Bayer – An ordered, grid-like dot matrix.
  • Floyd–Steinberg – Diffused, photographic grain.
  • Atkinson – Lighter diffusion with more contrast.
  • Halftone – Angled dots, like print screening.

Palettes

  • Auto – Colors sampled from your image.
  • Black & White – Pure black and white.
  • Game Boy – The four olive greens of the original 1989 Game Boy screen.
  • CGA – A 1980s PC-graphics look, built from that era's high-contrast four-color sets, such as black, cyan, magenta, and white.
  • Sepia – Warm brown tones, like an aged photograph.
  • Duotone – A custom pair of two colors that you pick.

Shimmer – With the Bayer and Halftone patterns, Animate Dither offsets the pattern across frames. This creates moving grain in the animated preview and in exports, even over a still image. It can increase export size.

Effects

Layer Effects

Layer effects apply to a single layer. They are Outline, Shadow, Bevel & Gloss, and Sparkles, on Sticker, Text, and Shape layers. Outline and Shadow are described in each layer type's section. Each effect card has its own Enabled toggle. Turning an effect off keeps its settings.

Reset effects disables every effect on the selected layer and clears the saved settings. It does not reset Animation, which has its own Motion group. To pixelate, posterize, or dither the picture, add a pixel filter layer.

Sparkles

Sparkles scatter small animated glints, stars, hearts, or snowflakes over a layer. They move with the layer and loop seamlessly in exported GIFs.

Sparkles effect – Turn on Sparkles for a sticker, text, shape, fill layer, frame, or the canvas, then pick a preset. Under Customize, set Place:

  • Scatter – Spreads sparkles over the layer.
  • Edges – Traces the layer's edges.
  • Highlights – Puts stars on the layer's bright areas (Kira Kira).

Sparkles layer – For weather in front of everything, click + in the Layers panel and choose Sparkles. The layer covers the whole picture and starts as Winter Snow. Its settings are in {ui-panel:sparkleLayerSettingsSection}. Fall and Rise wrap around the picture, so the snow never runs out.

Animation Beta

Animation adds one or more looping motions or transitions to any Glitter Fill, Sticker, Text, or Shape layer. Its card is at the bottom of the selected layer's properties. It is in Beta and still being refined.

Choose the motion – Enable Animation, then choose a preset as a starting point. Presets cover subtle idle movement, attention-grabbing motion, movement through space, and one-time transitions.

  • To stack another preset, open Customize and choose Add animation.
  • When a layer has several animations, select one at the top of the card to edit it.

Tune the result – Speed and Intensity are the main adjustments. Open Advanced for easing, direction, iterations, delay, start offset, fill mode, the shared layer anchor, and motion sampling. Orbit keeps a separate orbit center.

  • Timeline – Layer animations share one canvas timeline by default. Start offset shifts one layer around its loop by a percentage.
  • Iterations – Finite iterations play the effect a set number of times. Continuous motion loops while the layer is visible.
  • Pixel snap – Keeps pixel-art movement aligned to the pixel grid.

Path presets

  • Marquee – Fits its travel to the canvas and repeats copies, so one enters as another leaves, with a seamless wrap. Angle sets the direction.
  • Ricochet – Bounces between the canvas edges. Turns sets the number of round trips. Angle sets the direction and the balance of horizontal and vertical bounces. Bounce counts use whole round trips to keep the loop seamless.
  • Wander – Follows a stable looping path. Intensity sets its range. Ricochet and Wander are also Sparkles behaviors, where Wander gives particles a firefly motion.

Pause – To edit without motion, use Pause motion (see Canvas View Options). It does not change the export.

Settings

App Settings

Click the gear button in the main toolbar to change editor-wide preferences. Search by setting name or description to jump to a control.

Interface – Choose a theme, show or hide contextual hints, and decide whether destructive actions need confirmation.

Reset – Reset one settings group, or use Reset All Settings for interface, editing, brush, panel, and export preferences. Resetting settings does not change the current project or remove uploaded assets.

Editing & Workspace

Antialias Edges – Smooths the one-pixel rendered edge of text and shapes, and the edges of future strokes on Glitter Fill masks. It does not make Text or Shape layers paintable with the Glitter Brush. Brush Softness is a separate, wider feather.

Scale Outlines & Effects – Scales outline widths, dotted spacing, and shadow offsets when you resize Sticker, Text, or Shape layers. Outline and effect sizes are always in canvas pixels, so the panel shows the size that is drawn.

Transform Textures – Off by default. When off, resizing Text and Shape layers keeps the glitter texture's repeat size unchanged. Turn it on to scale the texture with the layer.

Reset Tools – Restores the saved Brush and Eraser tip, stroke, and pressure controls.

Reset Panels – Expands all collapsible property and tool groups.

Export

Exporting

Name the project – Type a name in the field at the top right of the app, beside Save and Export. On a phone it is at the top of the Menu. The name is used for exports and Save Project. If you leave it blank, Glitter uses the default file name.

Set the options – Open Export Settings from the toolbar or the export flow. See Export Settings.

Export – Click Export to generate an animated GIF. MP4 is also available when the browser supports video encoding. The result screen shows the file size, frame count, duration, and dimensions before you save, open, or share the file.

Save Project – Click Save Project or press Ctrl/CmdS to download a .glitter.json file. You can reopen it later with all layers, masks, text, shapes, and uploaded stickers intact.

Export Settings

Search by setting name or description, or scan the Output, Playback, Quality, and Optimization groups. Quality and Optimization show their main choices first. Use Customize to reveal the settings each choice controls. GIF and MP4 controls appear only when that format is active.

Output – Choose GIF or MP4, preserve transparency where supported, set the matte color used behind transparent areas, and optionally add the watermark. MP4 appears only when the device supports video encoding. MP4 always uses the matte color, because video does not support transparency.

Playback – Normal GIF timing is preserved. Fallback Frame Time applies only when a source has no usable timing data. MP4 can use an exact duration or stop after complete loops, and its estimated duration updates before you export.

Quality – Start with Clean · Automatic. It keeps the encoder's per-frame color selection and adds no export texture. The other GIF Look presets use a shared palette: 128 gives a classic-web balance, and 64 and 32 give progressively chunkier texture. The live preview shows the selected dither against gradients and hard edges. MP4 has Compact, Standard, and High bitrate presets.

  • Creative dithering – Comes from a Dither filter layer and is part of the artwork. It appears in the preview, in PNG-style rendering, and in GIF and MP4 exports, and may shimmer by design.
  • Export dithering – Happens afterward, only while fitting the finished artwork into a GIF palette. Keep it stable for clean animation, or animate Bayer or Halftone for deliberate noise.
  • Using both – Allowed. It adds a second, finer export texture. The result screen notes this, so double dithering is deliberate.
  • Texture Scale – Enlarges the pattern without lowering artwork resolution. Pixelate changes the artwork itself by making larger color cells.

Optimization – Export fidelity is a five-step choice, from an exact preview match to the smallest shareable file. Authored GIF timing is unchanged. Generated animation periods may be adjusted slightly so the loop closes cleanly on the composite loop. Every period change or removed frame is reported, and every reduction preserves the resolved loop length.

Customize exposes Motion Detail, Max frame difference, Frame Limit, Smart Frame Reduction, and manual frame skipping.

After export, expand Animation analysis to see the native frame timing of authored assets, the periods and cycle counts of generated effects, and the rendered frame count and sampling rate of the export.

Use an Export in an Instagram Story

There are two methods. The video method is simpler when the device supports MP4. The GIF method works on any device.

Video method – In Export Settings, choose MP4 and Exact duration, and enter the Story length you need, such as 15 seconds. Export, then save or share the video.

  • Instagram may recompress uploaded video, which can soften fine glitter texture and small details. Use the High quality preset if you need more detail.

GIF clipboard method

  1. Save the GIF to your device.
  2. Open the location where the GIF is saved and copy it to your clipboard.
  3. Open Instagram and start a new Story.
  4. Tap the Aa (Text) icon at the top of the screen.
  5. Tap inside the text field and paste the GIF.
  6. Resize, rotate, and position the GIF as you would any text or sticker.
  7. Add a GIF from Instagram's built-in GIPHY search (see the next paragraph).
  8. Post the Story as usual.

Important: Because of an Instagram bug, the Story does not animate unless it contains a GIF from Instagram's built-in GIPHY search. It animates in the preview before posting, but not after.

  • Tap the sticker icon (the smiley-face square).
  • Select GIF and add any GIF. It does not matter which one.
  • Layer it behind your Glitter graphic, or place it outside the bounds of the Story.

Tips & Performance

Quick Tips

Selection range – Adjust Threshold to narrow or widen the range of colors in a Glitter Fill selection. See Selection Options.

Layer isolation – Turn on Show Only Selected Layer below the preview. See Canvas View Options.

Auto-Select – Turn it off to choose a layer in the Layers panel and keep moving it as you drag across other artwork.

Export performance – More animated layers, and sources with different frame counts, need more frames to form a smooth shared loop. Large canvases and intricate designs can also export slowly on lower-powered devices. To speed up an export:

  • In Export Settings, lower Motion Detail.
  • Set a smaller Frame Limit.
  • Choose Keep Fewer Frames.
