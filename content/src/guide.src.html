Guide

Use this guide to make a glitter graphic with text, stickers, or a photo, then save an editable project and export the finished image. Glitter is free to use in your browser, and your pictures are processed on your device.

For individual controls, advanced effects, and keyboard shortcuts, use the {open:documentationModal}Documentation{/open}.

Getting Started

Start a Project

Start blank. Open the editor. To make a graphic from scratch, pick a size under Or start blank on the Welcome, or choose or start with a blank canvas… on the start screen. A small canvas is a good place to try a name, a sticker collage, or a web button.

Open an image. To decorate a picture instead, choose Open Image and pick a PNG, JPG, or GIF from your device. You can also drop an image onto the workspace. It becomes the Base Image, the bottom of your design.

Choose a background. Use Other size… for other canvas sizes and backgrounds. Choose a transparent background if you want a cutout graphic with no rectangle behind it.

Work on a phone. On a phone, the same tools and panels open in drawers. Close a drawer when you want more room to see your canvas.

Text

Add Glitter Text

Create text. Choose the {ui-tool:text} tool and click or tap on the canvas to create text. Type a name or a short message.

Choose a font. In {ui-panel:textSettingsSection}, choose a font and size. Try a bold font first so the sparkle has room to show.

Fill with glitter. Choose a glitter fill for the text and pick a tile from the Library. The Library is the collection of glitter tiles and stickers you can use in your design. Try a few tiles to see which one suits your lettering.

Add an outline. Add an outline if the letters blend into the background. You can change the words, font, or glitter again later.

{open:documentationModal}See the text and fill controls in Documentation{/open}.

Stickers

Decorate Your Graphic

Add a sticker. Open the Library, browse the stickers, and choose one to add it to your canvas. Try a star, a heart, or a frame around your text.

Move and resize. Choose {ui-tool:select} to move things. Drag the sticker into place and use its corner handles to resize it. Add more stickers the same way.

Use your own pictures. You can use your own pictures as stickers too. Once a canvas is open, dropping another image onto the workspace adds it as a Sticker layer.

Layers

Arrange Your Design

Understand layers. Your text, each sticker, and any glitter you add are separate layers. Think of layers as pieces of paper stacked on top of each other. Higher layers cover lower ones.

Change the stacking order. Choose an item in the Layers panel to edit it. Move layers in the list to change what sits in front. If a sticker covers your words, put the text above it.

Try a change. Use the visibility control to hide a layer while you try something else. If you make a change you dislike, use Undo. You can experiment without starting over.

Glitter

Add Glitter to a Photo

Pick a color. With a photo open, choose {ui-tool:glitterFill} and click a color in the picture that you want to sparkle. Pick a glitter tile for the new glitter layer.

Adjust the selection. If too much or too little of the picture is selected, adjust Threshold. A smaller value selects a narrower range of colors; a larger value includes more similar colors. Undo and try a different spot if you need to.

Tidy the edges. Use the brush and eraser to tidy the glittered area as you get more comfortable. The {open:documentationModal}Documentation{/open} explains selection options, painting, and Auto Glitter in detail.

Saving & Exporting

Keep Your Project and Share Your Graphic

Save an editable copy. Save Project keeps an editable copy. It downloads a .glitter.json file with your layers and settings. Keep that file so you can reopen your design and change it later.

Choose an export format. Export makes the finished picture you can post or send. Use an animated GIF to keep the glitter moving, or choose a still image in Export Settings for a picture without animation. Start with the default export settings while you learn.

Export the result. Give the project a name in the editor header, then choose Export. When it finishes, review the result and save or share the file. On a phone, the project name is in the Menu.

Keep both files. Save your editable project before closing the tab. An exported GIF or PNG is a finished image; the project file is what lets you come back to the separate pieces.

Use an Export in an Instagram Story

There are two methods. The video method is simpler when the device supports MP4. The GIF method works on any device.

Video method. In Export Settings, choose MP4 and Exact duration, and enter the Story length you need, such as 15 seconds. Export, then save or share the video.

  • Instagram may recompress uploaded video, which can soften fine glitter texture and small details. Use the High quality preset if you need more detail.

GIF clipboard method

  1. Save the GIF to your device.
  2. Open the location where the GIF is saved and copy it to your clipboard.
  3. Open Instagram and start a new Story.
  4. Tap the Aa (Text) icon at the top of the screen.
  5. Tap inside the text field and paste the GIF.
  6. Resize, rotate, and position the GIF as you would any text or sticker.
  7. Add a GIF from Instagram's built-in GIPHY search (see the next paragraph).
  8. Post the Story as usual.

Important: Because of an Instagram bug, the Story does not animate unless it contains a GIF from Instagram's built-in GIPHY search. It animates in the preview before posting, but not after.

  • Tap the sticker icon (the smiley-face square).
  • Select GIF and add any GIF. It does not matter which one.
  • Layer it behind your Glitter graphic, or place it outside the bounds of the Story.

Tips & Performance

Quick Tips

Selection range. Adjust Threshold to narrow or widen the range of colors in a Glitter Fill selection. See {open:documentationModal}Selection Options{/open}.

Layer isolation. Turn on Show Only Selected Layer below the preview. See {open:documentationModal}Canvas View Options{/open}.

Auto-Select. Turn it off to choose a layer in the Layers panel and keep moving it as you drag across other artwork.

Export performance. More animated layers, and sources with different frame counts, need more frames to form a smooth shared loop. Large canvases and intricate designs can also export slowly on lower-powered devices. To speed up an export:

  • In Export Settings, lower Motion Detail.
  • Set a smaller Frame Limit.
  • Choose Keep Fewer Frames.
