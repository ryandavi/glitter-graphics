This tutorial index, compiled by LadyWolfette from posts originally shared in graphics newsgroup communities, preserves many of the techniques that were developing just before glitter graphics took off. Before glitter specifically became the effect, people were starting to composite tiled animations into text, dingbats, and shapes and finding new uses for small animated patterns that had previously appeared as divider bars and other webpage decorations. These experiments expanded what tiles could be used for, encouraged people to make and share larger collections of them, and helped create the conditions that glitter graphics grew out of.

One of the communities where many of these methods were being worked out was the WebTV newsgroup Gypsy Jewels, formally <i>alt.discuss.clubs.public.hobby.collecting.gypsy_jewels</i>. The group was founded by webjewel, Nicolé, with a small group of friends and opened on April 13, 2001. Its members shared tutorials, graphics, tools, and techniques with one another, often building on methods that had already circulated through WebTV graphics communities. Among the people posting tutorials or resources there were Tiger Lilly, WIZZARD, Pat, Ginger, Kelli, G J, Kathy, Keechi, ~ BW ~, Pat~Ca, Moondoggee, Lee723, Watcher V, Janet, Karen, ~mslauralou~, Dan, also known as = DAN = and associated with Sparkelies, tencatsonedog, WILLOW, Barbara Hallmark Dee~, Sassy, ~Wolfy, webjewel/GyPsY_JeWeLs, Marie, ~Pink~™, and Tootsie~.

On February 4, 2002, Nicolé announced that she was leaving MSN TV and planned to close Gypsy Jewels a few days later so she could spend more time with her family. Her farewell shows how strongly the group understood itself as a collaborative learning community. She thanked members for the work and time they had given one another, wrote that the group had “made Dreams happen,” and encouraged them to keep helping people who wanted to learn, just as they had helped one another.

Rather than dispersing, much of the group moved almost immediately into a successor newsgroup. On February 7, 2002, ~mslauralou~ opened <i>alt.discuss.clubs.public.home.misc.mslauralou</i> and invited the Gypsy Jewels members to follow her there. The move preserved much of the same network of people and allowed the exchange of tutorials and graphics techniques to continue under a new group name, thankfully one without a slur, although it was a different time.

The methods developed in Gypsy Jewels and MsLauraLou spread far beyond those two Usenet groups. Tutorials continued circulating through other Usenet graphics communities and were reposted on personal websites, while the finished graphics themselves were copied, shared, and displayed across the web. As more PC users encountered these effects, some began working out how to reproduce them with the tools they already had. They could follow the same browser-based procedures WebTV users had developed, but they could also adapt the techniques to desktop programs such as Paint Shop Pro and Animation Shop and publish tutorials of their own.

As these methods moved between communities and software, animated tiles became material for increasingly elaborate effects rather than simply repeating webpage decorations. More tiles were made, collected, modified, and redistributed, but the sets created by Mica, Moon, Dan, and Aylana became some of the most widely circulated and recognizable. Their tiles appear repeatedly across later glitter collections and helped establish the visual vocabulary that came to define glitter graphics.
