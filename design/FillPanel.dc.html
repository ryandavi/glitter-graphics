Fill is the only slot with an Advanced disclosure. It splits into two labelled sets: Color adjust (Hue / Saturation / Brightness) and Texture position (Anchor + Offset), separated by a full-bleed hairline — texture coordinates are a different space from color. Anchor = the app's TextureAnchor Artwork / Canvas segmented. "Reset texture position" is the set-scoped revert on the Offset row. Border and Shadow have no Advanced.
