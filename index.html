Welcome

Glitter graphics are animated graphics characterized by looping sparkle and glitter effects applied to text, illustrations, or photographs. Popular online throughout the 2000s, they served as a means of personalizing early social media profiles and amateur websites.

This editor allows you to create glitter graphics using assets sourced from GeoCities, Angelfire, and Tripod archives.

Get Started

  • Upload an image as your base and using the glitter select tool, select colors to apply glitter effects
  • Add sticker layers and select stickers to place on the base image
  • Export your creation as an animated GIF
