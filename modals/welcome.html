Glitter graphics are animated graphics characterized by looping sparkle and glitter effects applied to text, illustrations, or photographs. Popular online throughout the 2000s, they served as a means of personalizing early social media profiles and amateur websites.

This editor allows you to create glitter graphics using assets sourced from GeoCities, Angelfire, and Tripod archives. Images, glitter fills, stickers, text, and shapes remain editable as layers while you arrange, transform, mask, and preview your work.

What you can make

  • Glitter fills — Apply animated glitter to selected image colors or a painted mask.
  • Text and shapes — Create editable type and shapes with glitter, solid, or gradient fills, borders, and shadows.
  • Stickers — Layer included graphics or upload your own images and animated GIFs.
  • Projects and exports — Save an editable project or export the finished animation as a GIF or MP4.

Getting started

  1. Choose a canvas. Upload an image or create a blank or transparent canvas.
  2. Build with layers. Add and arrange glitter fills, stickers, text, and shapes.
  3. Preview and export. Check the loop, then save your project or export the finished animation.
