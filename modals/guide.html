Getting Started

Load Your Image

Open an image – From the start screen, choose Open Image or drop a PNG, JPG, or GIF onto the workspace.

  • This becomes your Base Image – the foundation of your canvas for glitter effects and stickers.
  • If your image is larger than 1024 × 1024px, it will be resized to fit the constraints.
  • Before resizing, Glitter shows the resulting dimensions and lets you continue or cancel. Your original file is never changed.
  • Maximum file size: 10MB.
  • After a document is open, dropping another image onto the workspace adds it as a new Sticker layer instead of replacing your canvas.

Start blank – Click New Canvas, then choose from Social Media, Web Classics, or General presets—including the classic 400 × 400 Blingee size—or enter custom dimensions and choose a color or transparent background.

Open a project – Click Open Project on the start screen, or drag a .json project file onto the workspace to restore it.

Project preflight – Before replacing the current project, Glitter checks it and reports:

  • Unavailable stickers or fonts, and unknown layer types.
  • Files created by a newer editor version.

Review any substitutions or skipped content before choosing Open Anyway. When opening a newer file, avoid overwriting the original — unsupported data may be lost when saving.

Edit the canvas – Select the protected Base Image layer to open Canvas Properties.

  • Background – Choose Image, Transparent, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Replace is the explicit way to change the base image without disturbing other layers.
  • Image Size – Enter a linked pixel size or percentage to resize the canvas and every editable layer together. Choose whether glitter textures and visual effects scale with the design.
  • Canvas Size – Crop or extend without scaling content. Use Relative for pixel deltas, choose an anchor, and fill new canvas area with transparency or a color.

Basics

Tools

Some tools show context-specific options that appear only when certain elements or layers are selected.

Select Tool (V) – Pick layers and move stickers, text, and shapes. Turn off Auto-Select in the Select options when canvas drags should keep moving the layer already selected in the Layers panel. Nudge the selected layer with the arrow keys (hold Shift for 10px steps).

When a sticker, text, or shape layer is selected, alignment buttons appear:

  • Center layer horizontally
  • Center layer vertically

For multi-layer selection, cycling, and range-select, see Selection & Alignment.

Hand Tool (H) – Click and drag to pan around the canvas. Hold Space to use the Hand tool temporarily, then release it to return to the previous tool.

  • Touch – Use one or two fingers to pan from any tool; the separate Hand tool is hidden because it is redundant.

Context toolbar alignment options:

  • Center canvas horizontally
  • Center canvas vertically

Color Fill (I) – With a glitter layer or base image selected, click on the canvas to create a color selection based on the base image.

Mask Brush (B) – Paint or erase the mask of a Glitter Fill layer directly on the canvas. Painting while another layer type is selected creates a separate Glitter Fill layer.

  • See Mask Brush under Glitter for painting modes and brush settings.

Text (T) – Click the canvas to place Point text, or drag to define a Box that wraps text inside a resizable frame.

  • See Text for typography, layout, and fill/border/shadow options.

Shape (U) – Drag on the canvas to draw a shape at that size (Shift = square), or click for a default size.

  • See Shapes for shape choices and fill/border/shadow options.

Zoom Tool (Z) – Click to zoom in, Alt-click to zoom out, or drag right/up and left/down for smooth scrubby zoom. Double-click the Zoom tool to return to 100%.

  • Touch – Two-finger pinch to zoom.
  • Trackpad – Pinch to zoom smoothly around the gesture position; swipe with two fingers to pan in any direction.
  • Mouse – Use Space-drag or middle-button drag to pan from any tool. Double-click the Hand tool to fit the canvas.

Context toolbar zoom options:

  • Reset – Double-click the status-bar zoom percentage to reset zoom to 100%.
  • Fit – Zoom to fit the entire image on screen
  • Fill – Zoom to fill screen with image
  • Zoom to Selection – Press Shift2 to center and fit the selected layer or group.

Working with Layers

The Layer Panel lets you manage all layers.

Click + to add a new Glitter Fill, Sticker, Text, or Shape layer.

Drag layers up/down to reorder them (top layers appear in front).

  • Touch – Use the handles to drag layers.

Move, resize & rotate. Drag inside the box to move, use the corner handles to resize or scale, and use the top handle to rotate. Side handles scale stickers and shapes along one axis. For text, Point mode handles scale the text while Box mode handles resize the frame without changing the type size.

  • Rotation snapping – Hold Shift while rotating (with the handle or the Rotation slider) to snap to 15° increments.
  • Axis-lock – Hold Shift while dragging to constrain movement to one axis.
  • Lock Aspect – When enabled, the corner and circular middle-edge handles preserve the layer's current aspect ratio.

Transform card. Enter exact X/Y, W/H, rotation, and opacity, plus alignment, flip, and reset controls.

  • Nudge – Arrow keys move the selected layer by 1px (hold Shift for 10px).
  • Field stepping – The X/Y/W/H fields step with ↑/↓ (1px), or hold Shift for 10px.

Multiple layers. When several movable layers are selected, a shared bounding box appears — drag inside it to move the group, or use the corner handles to scale it uniformly. See Selection & Alignment for building a selection.

  • Touch – Taps select the topmost layer; add or remove layers from the Layers panel, then move or pinch the shared group box on the canvas.

Click the eye icon to show/hide layers.

Click the lock icon to prevent canvas transforms and property changes while keeping the layer available for selection and inspection. The Base layer remains editable because its lock means fixed to the canvas rather than read-only.

Click the duplicate icon to copy a layer with all its settings.

Click the trash icon to delete a layer.

Selection & Alignment

Work with several layers at once, and use shortcuts to speed up common edits.

Making a selection

  • Marquee – Drag on empty canvas to select the layers it touches. Hold Shift while dragging to add to the current selection.
  • On the canvas – Shift-click toggles a layer under the pointer; hold Alt and click to cycle through overlapping layers.
  • In the Layers panel – ShiftClick selects the whole range; CtrlClick/CmdClick toggles any single layer, including locked, Base Image, and Glitter Fill layers.
  • CtrlA/CmdA selects every movable layer; Escape clears the selection.

A selection that includes a protected or non-transformable layer — locked, Base Image, or Glitter Fill — stays selectable but cannot move as a group.

Editing shortcuts

Open Commands & Shortcuts from the header. Use the Keyboard and Canvas Gestures tabs to browse task-focused groups, or search by command, key, device, gesture, or section. Keyboard bindings use native Mac or Windows labels automatically; searches still treat Command/Control and Option/Alt as equivalents.

Tool keys: Select Tool (V), Text Tool (T), Shape Tool (U), Color Fill Tool (I), Mask Brush Tool (B), Mask Eraser Tool (E), Hand Tool (H), and Zoom Tool (Z).

Brush keys: Swap Paint/Erase (Mask Brush) with X; Decrease Brush Size with [; Increase Brush Size with ]. Hold Shift for larger steps.

View keys: Fit Screen with Ctrl/Cmd + 0; Reset Zoom (100%) with Ctrl/Cmd + 1; Zoom In or Zoom Out with Ctrl/Cmd + +/−.

Layer keys: Select All Movable Layers with Ctrl/Cmd + A; Delete Selected Layer(s) with Delete/Backspace; Duplicate Selected Layer(s) with Ctrl/Cmd + D.

Clipboard keys: Copy Selected Layer(s) with Ctrl/Cmd + C. Ctrl/Cmd + V pastes copied layers or an image from the clipboard.

File key: Save Project with Ctrl/Cmd + S.

  • Duplicate – Hold Alt while dragging, or use CtrlD/CmdD.
  • Scale from center – Hold Alt while resizing.
  • Edit or rename – Double-click a text layer to edit its copy, or double-click a layer name to rename it.
  • Pan – Hold Space temporarily.
  • Escape – Closes an open picker or cancels an in-progress transform before it clears the selection.
  • Undo – CtrlZ/CmdZ.
  • Redo – CtrlY/CmdY or CtrlShiftZ/CmdShiftZ.

Smart Guides & Snapping – While moving a sticker, text, shape, or multi-layer selection, the editor snaps its edges and center to the canvas and to visible layers. Pink guide lines appear at the active alignment.

  • Snapping is on by default. Use Snapping below the preview to turn it on or off.
  • Hold Ctrl during a move to temporarily disable snapping without changing the toggle.
  • Smart Guides currently assist movement only; resizing and rotation remain freeform, apart from Shift rotation snapping.

Align & distribute

  • Pick Selection or Canvas as what to align to.
  • Align left / center / right, or top / middle / bottom.
  • With three or more layers, distribute them horizontally or vertically.
  • Duplicate and Delete are under Actions.

Canvas View Options

Control what you see on the canvas.

Toggle Bounds – Outline the canvas boundary and clip artwork that extends beyond it, so the preview shows what is inside the exported canvas.

Toggle Transparency – Show/hide the checkerboard pattern behind transparent areas.

Show Only Selected Layer – Temporarily isolate the selected layer on the canvas. Click again to show every visible layer. This changes only the canvas view, not the export or layer visibility settings.

Mobile

On phones and tablets, the same start screen fills the workspace. Open an image, create a preset canvas, or open a project; after that, the desktop panels become drawers and a bottom sheet.

Drawers – Three tabs hold what the desktop panels show:

  • Design – The gallery of glitter, stickers, and shapes.
  • Layers – Stacking order and selection.
  • Edit – Properties for the active layer.

Edit sheet – Drag the sheet's handle to expand it, or swipe down to close.

Gestures – On touch, pan with one or two fingers from any tool and pinch to zoom. On a trackpad, swipe with two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom. Drag layers by their handles in the Layers drawer; a tap selects the topmost layer.

App Settings – The header gear button. Clear All is in the Layers drawer action bar.

About and Guide – Use the compact navigation bar to search the document, open its Contents, or return to the top. On phones, both open as matching near-full sheets with a small frame around the dialog.

Effects

Canvas Effects

Effects are non-destructive — turning one off keeps its settings, and each card has its own Enabled toggle. There are two kinds:

  • Canvas effects apply to the whole Base Image — the Pixelate and Palette pipeline described below.
  • Layer effects apply to a single layer — Shadow on Sticker layers, and Border and Shadow on Text and Shape layers, covered in each layer's own section.

Reset Effects disables every effect on the selected canvas or layer and clears those saved settings. Canvas effects run in a fixed pipeline, each stage feeding the next:

Pixelate → Palette → Color Adjust → Opacity

  • Pixelate – Groups pixels into crisp mosaic cells.
  • Palette – Reduces colors, either by flattening connected regions with Posterize or approximating them with Dither.
  • Color Adjust – Shifts hue, saturation, and brightness.
  • Opacity – Fades the whole treated image.

Posterize – Flattens the image into a set number of solid color regions.

  • Colors – How many colors the palette is reduced to.
  • Cleanup – An optional disclosure with Detail and Clean Edges to tidy small specks and jagged region boundaries.

Dither – Approximates colors with a repeating pattern instead of flat regions. Pick a pattern style and a palette, then use Strength to control how pronounced the pattern is.

Pattern styles:

  • Bayer – Ordered, grid-like dot matrix.
  • Floyd–Steinberg – Diffused, photographic grain.
  • Atkinson – Lighter diffusion with more contrast.
  • Halftone – Angled dots, like print screening.

Palettes:

  • Auto – Colors sampled from your own image.
  • Black & White – Pure black and white, two-tone.
  • Game Boy – The four shades of olive green from the original 1989 Game Boy's screen.
  • CGA – A vivid 1980s PC-graphics look, built from that era's high-contrast four-color sets (such as black, cyan, magenta, and white).
  • Sepia – Warm brown tones, like an aged photograph.
  • Duotone – A custom two-color pair you pick.

Shimmer – For the Bayer and Halftone patterns, offsets the pattern across frames to create living grain in both the live preview and the exported GIF. It can increase export size.

Unlike Auto Glitter, these effects stay a single editable Base Image treatment and never create layers.

Auto Glitter

Auto Glitter scans your base image and turns its colors into real animated glitter fills. It is available when Canvas Background is set to Image, and opens the Auto Glitter panel, which previews live animated glitter directly on the canvas.

A temporary mode. While Auto Glitter is active, a compact Auto Glitter Mode strip sits above the navigation controls to mark the temporary mode. It shows status only while analyzing, updating, or reporting an error, and offers Exit to discard the session.

  • Navigation stays available – The tool switches to Hand; Hand, Zoom, mouse-wheel, Space-drag, and touch pan/pinch all work.
  • Select a canvas match – Switch to Select, then choose visible glitter on the canvas to open that Color Match in the Design gallery.
  • Editing is locked – Regular layer selection, undo/redo, and the editing tools stay locked until you create or discard the preview.

Compare the result. Switch Original / Flat / Glitter at the top of the panel to compare the source image, the flattened posterized colors, and the final animated fills. The create and cancel actions stay pinned at the bottom while Palette, Color Matches, and Advanced scroll between them.

When an Auto Glitter set already exists, choose Edit to reopen its visible regions and swatches, Replace to build a fresh replacement, or Add to keep it and create an additional set. Changing Palette or Advanced while editing rebuilds the current set from the Base Image.

Palette. Colors and Combine Similar update the live result instantly, without repeating the expensive image scan. Choose an overall look:

  • Vibrant – Prioritizes colorful accents and combines more neutral shades.
  • Balanced – Keeps more light and shadow structure.
  • Natural – Stays closest to the photo's color distribution.

Color Matches. Each detected color appears as a match. Select a match swatch to open the Design gallery — gallery choices update the live preview in place, and Done returns to Auto Glitter. Until you pick a swatch, the gallery stays in an instructional empty state; while its picker is open, your choice updates only that one match.

  • Skip a color – Use a match's eye button to leave that color out.
  • Combine colors – Drag one color row onto another. On touch devices, drag with the row handle.

Advanced. Open Advanced to change the minimum region detail, clean up anti-aliased edges and dither noise, or turn off matched-glitter hue tuning.

Create N Layers commits a new preview as normal, editable Glitter Fill layers. When reopening a set, Apply Changes replaces that set in place. Transparent areas of the image stay transparent, the Base Image is left unchanged, and your previous tool is restored.

  • Canvas Effects – Any active canvas effects are turned off after creation, so the covered Base Image isn't processed during export. Their settings are preserved and the status bar notes the change.
  • Re-running – Auto Glitter can replace the latest generated set or keep it and add another. If you have edited the generated layers, it defaults to the safer keep-and-add.

Discarding. Cancel, Exit, Escape, replacing the Base Image, or leaving the panel discards the live preview. Leaving before the layers are created asks for confirmation first.

Glitter

Create a Glitter Layer

In the Layer Panel, click + and choose Glitter Fill.

  • This creates a new layer to add glitter effects.

Choose Your Glitter

Browse the glitter gallery and click any glitter to apply it to your selected colors.

  • Use search and filters to find specific styles, colors, or categories.

Select Colors

Use the Color Fill Tool (I) to click any color in your base image.

  • This tells the glitter layer which parts to fill.
  • A glitter layer will be empty until a selection is made.
  • When a selection is made, the fill will be outlined in blue to better visualize the area (desktop only, it's too resource intensive on mobile). This outline is only visible in the preview.

Selection Options

Multi-Select – Make multiple selections on the same layer.

Threshold – Sets the tolerance for color matching when selecting areas to fill with glitter.

  • Lower values – Selects colors very close to the one clicked for precise selections.
  • Higher values – Selects a broader range of similar colors across the entire image.

Feather – Softens the edges of your selection for smoother blending.

Contiguous – Only selects connected areas of the same color, preventing scattered pixel selection.

Invert – Flip the completed layer mask so glitter covers everything except the selected and painted areas.

Adjust Glitter Properties

In the Glitter Properties panel, customize your glitter layer:

Use Change in the Fill asset card to open the glitter gallery for the selected layer. The picker strip identifies the destination; choose glitter, then press Done to return to Glitter Properties.

Opacity – Controls transparency (100% = fully visible, 0% = invisible).

Sources & Advanced – Fill slots can use Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Expand Advanced for Scale (glitter pattern size), Hue, Saturation, and Brightness, plus texture anchoring and offsets. These controls also appear on the applicable Sticker, Text, Shape, and Canvas Background slots and match in exports.

Texture Scale, Opacity, Threshold, and Feather are adjusted independently for each glitter fill layer.

Mask Brush

Beyond color selections, use the Mask Brush to manually paint or erase exactly where a Glitter Fill layer appears. Text and Shape layers use their own rendered outlines and cannot hold painted masks.

Click the Mask Brush in the toolbar, or press B, to start painting. If a Glitter Fill layer is selected, the stroke edits that layer. Otherwise, the first painted stroke creates a new Glitter Fill layer. Erasing requires an existing Glitter Fill layer.

Paint / Erase – Use the Eraser tool in the toolbar (press E, or X to swap between Brush and Eraser) to remove glitter with each stroke instead of adding it — including areas added by color selection. On a pen tablet, flipping to the pen's eraser end erases automatically for that stroke.

Shape, Size, Softness, Flow, Spacing, and Smoothing – Pick the brush tip (round, square, calligraphy, star, or heart), then set its diameter, edge feathering, how much glitter builds up per stroke, how far apart the stamps sit along the stroke, and how much shaky strokes are stabilized. The Brush and Eraser keep their own independent settings and are remembered between sessions.

  • Resize – Use [ and ] to change brush size, or hold Shift for larger jumps.

Straight lines – Hold Shift while dragging to constrain the current stroke to a straight line (0°, 45°, or 90°).

Brush Settings and Eraser Settings organize controls into collapsible Brush Tip, Stroke, and Actions groups. Pressure Sensitivity sits with the Stroke dynamics it modifies. Actions can reset the active tool, copy the other tool's current settings, or Clear Paint while keeping color selections intact.

Invert – Flip the mask so glitter covers everything except what's been selected and painted.

Clear Paint – Remove all painted strokes while keeping color selections intact.

Toggle Mask Overlay in the preview controls to see painted areas highlighted while you work.

Stickers

Add Stickers

Create a Sticker layer to add decorative images on top of glitter.

Add the layer – Click + in the Layer Panel and choose Sticker.

  • The sticker layer will be empty until a sticker is chosen.

Pick a sticker – Browse the gallery and click one to add it.

Position it – Use the Select Tool (V) to drag and move the sticker.

Sticker Properties

Opacity – Control transparency.

Transform – Enter exact X/Y position, W/H size, rotation, and opacity, or use the alignment, flip, and reset controls.

Handles – Drag corner handles to scale proportionally, or use side handles to resize one axis.

Lock Aspect – Keep width and height proportional while resizing, or turn it off to stretch or squash the sticker.

Flip Horizontally/Vertically – Mirror the sticker.

Change – Use Change in the Asset card to swap in a different sticker without recreating the layer.

Advanced – Expand the Asset card’s color adjustments for Hue, Saturation, and Brightness. Each control can be reset independently.

Shadow – An optional Shadow paint slot with the same sources as text and shapes: glitter (with its own swatch plus Advanced Hue / Saturation / Brightness), solid color, or gradient.

Actions – Fit canvas scales and centers the sticker to fit fully inside the canvas; Fill canvas scales it to cover the canvas completely.

Text

Create a Text Layer

Click + in the Layer Panel and choose Text, or press T and click (or drag) on the canvas.
  • Clicking places Point text that hugs the glyphs; dragging defines a Box that wraps text inside a resizable frame.
Browse the glitter gallery and click any glitter to fill the text with it, just like a glitter layer.

Typography & Layout

In the Text Properties panel, edit the copy, choose a non-destructive letter case, and control how it is laid out. Double-click or double-tap text to open and select its copy.

Text – Type your copy directly into the text field.

Font – Choose from the built-in font picker.

Font Size and Letter Spacing – Adjust glyph size and the gap between characters.

Point / Box – Point text hugs the copy and scales with the corner handles. Box text wraps inside a frame you can resize with the edge handles.

Line Height – Adjust the spacing between lines when text wraps inside a Box. Not shown for Point text, which is always a single line.

Actions – Use Fit to Text to resize a Box text frame to its current content. It is only available while the layer is in Box mode.

Vertical and Horizontal alignment – Position text within a Box frame (top/middle/bottom) and align lines (left/center/right).

Fill, Border & Shadow

Each part of the text keeps its own source — glitter, solid color, or gradient, with None also available for the fill.

Fill – Choose None, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Texture Scale appears for glitter, and Opacity applies to visible sources.

Border – Enable an outline around the text and set its width. Choose Glitter, Solid, or Gradient, then control rounded/hard edges, outside/on-edge/inside placement, and whether the border sits behind or on top of the fill.

Shadow – Enable a drop shadow and set its X/Y offset. Like the border, it can use its own glitter, solid color, or gradient.

Only one swatch is "active" for glitter picking at a time — click a swatch (Fill, Border, or Shadow) to send the next glitter you pick in the gallery to that part of the text.

Shapes

Draw a Shape

Add clean vector shapes filled with glitter, solid color, or a gradient. The Design gallery shows every shape at once, organized under Basic Shapes, Stars & Bursts, Hearts & Flowers, Nature & Weather, Arrows & Banners, Speech & Music, and Symbols & Signs. Choices include polygons, stars, hearts, flowers, weather icons, arrows, speech bubbles, music notes, identity symbols, and common signs.

Click the Shape tool in the toolbar, or press U. Drag on the canvas to draw the shape at that size (hold Shift to keep it square or circular), or just click for a default-size shape.

Add shapes from the Shapes area of the Design gallery. Scroll through the category headings — there is no extra category screen to open. If a shape layer is already selected, choosing a gallery shape replaces that layer's shape; otherwise it creates a new shape layer. Shape Properties controls the selected shape's fill, transform, border, and shadow. Resize, rotate, and move it with the transform handles — shapes re-render crisply at their new size.

Fill, Border & Shadow

Each shape has a Fill plus optional Border and Shadow, and every one can be glitter, a solid color, or a gradient.

Fill – Choose None, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Set the fill to None with a Border on to get an outline shape. (The same None option on text makes outlined text.)

Border & Shadow – Enable each and pick its own glitter, solid color, or gradient, plus width/offset and opacity. Borders follow the shape's true outline, can be solid or dotted, and expose rounded/hard edges, outside/on-edge/inside placement, plus behind/on-top layering. Open Advanced on any slot to recolor its glitter (Hue / Saturation / Brightness).

Settings

App Settings

Open the gear button in the main toolbar to change editor-wide preferences. Search by setting name or description to jump straight to a control.

Interface – Choose a theme, show or hide contextual hints, control whether Welcome opens at startup, and decide whether destructive actions need confirmation.

Reset – Reset one settings group or use Reset All Settings for interface, editing, brush, panel, and export preferences. Resetting settings does not change the current project or remove uploaded assets.

Editing & Workspace

Antialias Edges – Smooth the one-pixel rendered edge of text and shapes, plus the edges of future strokes on Glitter Fill masks. It does not make Text or Shape layers paintable with the Mask Brush. Brush Softness remains a separate, wider feather.

Scale Borders & Effects – Scale border widths, dotted spacing, and shadow offsets when Text or Shape layers are resized.

Transform Textures – Scale glitter texture size with Text and Shape layers. Turn it off to resize the artwork while keeping the texture repeat size unchanged.

Reset Tools restores saved Brush and Eraser tip, stroke, and pressure controls. Reset Panels expands all collapsible property and tool groups.

Export

Exporting

When ready to save, export your design.

Use the Project section in the Canvas panel to name your project. That name is reused for exports and Save Project; leave it blank to use the default file name.

Open Export Settings from the toolbar or export flow to customize output options.

Click Export to generate an animated GIF. MP4 is also available when the browser supports video encoding.

Click Save Project or press CtrlS/CmdS to download a .glitter.json file you can reopen later with all layers, masks, text, shapes, and uploaded stickers intact.

Export Settings

Search by setting name or description, or scan the always-visible Output, Playback, Quality, and Optimization groups. Optimization is labeled Advanced, but its controls are not hidden behind a disclosure. GIF- and MP4-specific controls appear only when their format is active.

Output – Choose GIF or MP4, preserve transparency where supported, set the matte color used behind transparent areas, and optionally add the watermark. MP4 appears only when the device supports video encoding and always uses the matte color because video does not support transparency.

Playback – Fallback Frame Time is used only when a source has no usable timing metadata; normal GIF timing is preserved. MP4 can use an exact duration or stop after complete loops, and its estimated duration updates before export.

Quality – Color Quality preserves subtler differences at the cost of export time. Dithering reduces GIF color banding but is disabled with GIF transparency so transparent pixels remain reliable. MP4 adds Compact, Standard, and High bitrate presets.

Optimization – Frame Limit targets animation complexity, Optimization Goal balances motion fidelity against file size, Smart Frame Reduction combines visually identical frames, Keep Fewer Frames applies a manual reduction, and Motion Detail controls how often fast changes are sampled. These controls preserve the animation's playback length.

After export, the result screen shows file size, frame count, duration, and dimensions before you save, open, or share the file.

Use an Export in an Instagram Story

Video method

If MP4 is available on the device, choose Exact duration and enter the Story length you need, such as 15 seconds, then export and save or share the video. Instagram may recompress uploaded video, which can soften fine glitter texture or small details; use the High quality preset if the result needs more detail.

GIF clipboard method

Save the GIF to your device.

Open the location where the GIF is saved, then copy it to your clipboard.

Open Instagram and start a new Story.

Tap the Aa (Text) icon at the top of the screen.

Tap within the text field, then paste the GIF from your clipboard.

Resize, rotate, and position the GIF like you would with any text or sticker.

THIS STEP IS IMPORTANT: Due to a bug with Instagram Stories, you MUST add a GIF from the GIPHY search built into Instagram or else the story will not animate, even though it will animate in the preview before posting.

  • Tap the sticker icon (smiley face square).
  • Select GIF, then add any GIF—it doesn't matter which one. Unless you want it there, then choose what you want.
  • Layer it behind your Glitter Graphic or place it outside the bounds of the story.

Post the damn story as normal.

Tips & Performance

Quick Tips

Selection range – Adjust Threshold to narrow or widen the range of colors included in a Glitter Fill selection.

Layer isolation – Enable Show Only Selected Layer below the preview. It changes only the workspace view, not the export.

Auto-Select – Turn it off when you want to choose a layer in the Layers panel and keep moving that selection as you drag across other artwork.

Export performance – More animated layers—and sources with different frame counts—require more frames to form a smooth shared loop.

  • Use Export Settings to lower Motion Detail, set a smaller Frame Limit, or choose Keep Fewer Frames.
  • Large canvases and intricate designs can also take longer on lower-powered devices.
