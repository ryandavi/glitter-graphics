Getting Started

Load Your Image

Drag an image onto the dropzone — or click it to browse — to load one (PNG, JPG, or GIF).
  • This becomes your Base Image – the foundation of your canvas for glitter effects and stickers.
  • If your image is larger than 1024×1024, it will be resized to fit the constraints.
  • Maximum file size: 10MB.
No image? Click New Blank Canvas to start from an empty canvas instead — choose its width, height, and background color.
Have a saved project? Click Open Project in the welcome screen, or drag a .json project file onto the main dropzone to restore it.
Resize the canvas anytime: with no layer selected, open the Design panel and use Canvas Size to enter new dimensions and pick an anchor. Your content keeps its size and position while the canvas is cropped or extended around it (extended areas stay transparent). A live outline previews the new bounds, and you can undo the change.

Basics

Tools

Some tools show context-specific options that appear only when certain elements or layers are selected.

Select Tool (V) – Pick layers and move stickers, text, and shapes. Nudge the selected layer with the arrow keys (hold Shift for 10px steps).

When a sticker, text, or shape layer is selected, alignment buttons appear:

  • Center layer horizontally
  • Center layer vertically
  • Hold Shift and click layers on the canvas or in the layer list to add or remove movable layers from the selection.
  • Hold Alt and click to cycle through overlapping layers under the pointer.

Hand Tool (H) – Click and drag to pan around the canvas.

  • On touch devices, use one or two fingers to pan

Context toolbar alignment options:

  • Center canvas horizontally
  • Center canvas vertically

Color Fill (I) – With a glitter layer or base image selected, click on the canvas to create a color selection based on the base image.

Mask Brush (B) – With a glitter or text layer selected, paint or erase glitter directly on the canvas.

  • See Mask Brush under Glitter for painting modes and brush settings.

Text (T) – Click the canvas to place Point text, or drag to define a Box that wraps text inside a resizable frame.

  • See Text for typography, layout, and fill/border/shadow options.

Shape (U) – Drag on the canvas to draw a shape at that size (Shift = square), or click for a default size.

  • See Shapes for shape choices and fill/border/shadow options.

Zoom Tool (Z) – To zoom in, click the canvas.

  • On touch devices, use two-finger pinch to zoom.
  • On trackpads, hold Ctrl and use the wheel gesture to zoom smoothly.

Context toolbar zoom options:

  • Click the current zoom percentage to reset zoom to 100%
  • Fit – Zoom to fit the entire image on screen
  • Fill – Zoom to fill screen with image

Working with Layers

The Layer Panel lets you manage all layers.

Click + to add a new Glitter Fill, Sticker, Text, or Shape layer.

Drag layers up/down to reorder them (top layers appear in front).

  • On touch devices, use the handles to drag layers.

Sticker, text, and shape layers have transform handles: drag to move, corner handles to resize or scale, side handles resize box text and shapes, and the top handle rotates.

  • Hold Shift while rotating — with the handle or the Rotation slider — to snap to 15° increments.
  • Hold Shift while dragging to axis-lock movement. The Transform card also gives exact X/Y, W/H, rotation, opacity, alignment, flip, and reset controls. Arrow keys nudge the selected layer (Shift = 10px).
  • The X/Y/W/H fields support keyboard stepping too: use ↑ or ↓ for 1px changes, or hold Shift for 10px steps.
  • When multiple movable layers are selected, a shared bounding box appears. Drag inside it to move the group, or use the corner handles to scale the group uniformly.
  • On touch, taps still select the topmost layer. To add or remove layers from the selection, use the layer list, then move or pinch the shared group box on the canvas.

Click the eye icon to show/hide layers.

Click the lock icon to prevent changes.

Click the duplicate icon to copy a layer with all its settings.

Click the trash icon to delete a layer.

Canvas View Options

Control what you see on the canvas.

Toggle Bounds – Show/hide bounding boxes around stickers.

Toggle Transparency – Show/hide the checkerboard pattern behind transparent areas.

Show Only Active Layer – Isolate the currently selected layer.

Glitter

Create a Glitter Layer

In the Layer Panel, click + and choose Glitter Fill.

  • This creates a new layer to add glitter effects.

Choose Your Glitter

Browse the glitter gallery and click any glitter to apply it to your selected colors.

  • Use search and filters to find specific styles, colors, or categories.

Select Colors

Use the Color Fill Tool (I) to click any color in your base image.

  • This tells the glitter layer which parts to fill.
  • A glitter layer will be empty until a selection is made.
  • When a selection is made, the fill will be outlined in blue to better visualize the area (desktop only, it's too resource intensive on mobile). This outline is only visible in the preview.

Selection Options

Multi-Select – Make multiple selections on the same layer.

Threshold – Sets the tolerance for color matching when selecting areas to fill with glitter.

  • Lower values – Selects colors very close to the one clicked for precise selections.
  • Higher values – Selects a broader range of similar colors across the entire image.

Feather – Softens the edges of your selection for smoother blending.

Contiguous – Only selects connected areas of the same color, preventing scattered pixel selection.

Invert – Select everything except the color clicked.

Adjust Glitter Properties

In the Glitter Properties panel, customize your glitter layer:

Opacity – Controls transparency (100% = fully visible, 0% = invisible).

Advanced – Expand for Scale (glitter pattern size) plus Hue, Saturation, and Brightness to recolor any glitter swatch without swapping it. Works on fill layers and on the text Fill, Border, and Shadow slots, and matches in the exported GIF.

Apply Globally – Apply scale, opacity, threshold, and feather settings across all glitter layers.

Mask Brush

Beyond color selections, use the Mask Brush to manually paint or erase exactly where glitter appears — available for glitter fill and text layers.

Click the Mask Brush in the toolbar, or press B, to start painting.

Paint / Erase – Use the Eraser tool in the toolbar (press E, or X to swap between Brush and Eraser) to remove glitter with each stroke instead of adding it — including areas added by color selection. On a pen tablet, flipping to the pen's eraser end erases automatically for that stroke.

Shape, Size, Softness, Flow, Spacing, and Smoothing – Pick the brush tip (round, square, calligraphy, star, or heart), then set its diameter, edge feathering, how much glitter builds up per stroke, how far apart the stamps sit along the stroke, and how much shaky strokes are stabilized. The Brush and Eraser keep their own independent settings and are remembered between sessions.

  • Use [ and ] to change brush size, or hold Shift for larger jumps.

Straight lines – Hold Shift while dragging to constrain the current stroke to a straight line (0°, 45°, or 90°).

Actions – Brush Settings and Eraser Settings each include an action to copy the other tool's current settings, plus Clear Paint to remove painted strokes while keeping your color selections intact.

Invert – Flip the mask so glitter covers everything except what's been selected and painted.

Clear Paint – Remove all painted strokes while keeping color selections intact.

Toggle Mask Overlay in the preview controls to see painted areas highlighted while you work.

Stickers

Add Stickers

Create a Sticker layer to add decorative images on top of glitter.

Click + in the Layer Panel and choose Sticker.
  • The sticker layer will be empty until a sticker is chosen.
Browse the sticker gallery and click one to add it.
Use the Select Tool (V) to drag and move the sticker.

Sticker Properties

Opacity – Control transparency.

Rotation – Spin the sticker (–180° to 180°).

Scale X & Y – Resize width and height independently.

Proportional Scale – When enabled, X and Y scale together to maintain aspect ratio. Disable to stretch or squash the sticker.

Flip Horizontally/Vertically – Mirror the sticker.

Text

Create a Text Layer

Click + in the Layer Panel and choose Text, or press T and click (or drag) on the canvas.
  • Clicking places Point text that hugs the glyphs; dragging defines a Box that wraps text inside a resizable frame.
Browse the glitter gallery and click any glitter to fill the text with it, just like a glitter layer.

Typography & Layout

In the Text Properties panel, edit the copy and how it's laid out.

Text – Type your copy directly into the text field.

Font – Choose from the built-in font picker.

Font Size and Letter Spacing – Adjust glyph size and the gap between characters.

Point / Box – Point text hugs the copy and scales with the corner handles. Box text wraps inside a frame you can resize with the edge handles.

Line Height – Adjust the spacing between lines when text wraps inside a Box. Not shown for Point text, which is always a single line.

Actions – Use Fit to Text to resize a Box text frame to its current content. It is only available while the layer is in Box mode.

Vertical and Horizontal alignment – Position text within a Box frame (top/middle/bottom) and align lines (left/center/right).

Fill, Border & Shadow

Each part of the text keeps its own source — glitter or solid color.

Fill – Texture Scale and Opacity control the glitter pattern applied to the letters themselves. Click the fill swatch to pick a different glitter.

Border – Enable an outline around the text and set its width. It defaults to glitter — click the border swatch to choose which one, or switch it to Solid Color for a flat color instead.

Shadow – Enable a drop shadow and set its X/Y offset. Like the border, it can use its own glitter or a solid color.

Only one swatch is "active" for glitter picking at a time — click a swatch (Fill, Border, or Shadow) to send the next glitter you pick in the gallery to that part of the text.

Shapes

Draw a Shape

Add clean vector shapes — circle, square, triangle, diamond, pentagon, hexagon, star, or heart — filled with glitter or solid color.

Click the Shape tool in the toolbar, or press U. Drag on the canvas to draw the shape at that size (hold Shift to keep it square or circular), or just click for a default-size shape.

Pick the shape from the gallery in Shape Properties. Resize, rotate, and move it with the transform handles like any other layer — shapes re-render crisply at their new size, so they never look pixelated.

Fill, Border & Shadow

Each shape has a Fill plus optional Border and Shadow, and every one can be glitter or a solid color.

Fill – Choose None, Glitter, or Solid. Set the fill to None with a Border on to get an outline shape. (The same None option on text makes outlined text.)

Border & Shadow – Enable each and pick its own glitter or solid color, width/offset, and opacity. Borders follow the shape's true outline, so even sharp shapes like the star stay smooth. Shape borders can also switch to Dotted Border mode with adjustable dot spacing. Open Advanced on any slot to recolor its glitter (Hue / Saturation / Brightness).

Export

Exporting

When ready to save, export your design.

Use the Project section in the Canvas panel to name your project. That name is reused for both Export GIF and Save Project; leave it blank to use the default file name.

Open Export Settings from the toolbar or export flow to customize output options.

Click Export to generate your animated GIF.

Click Save Project or press Ctrl + S to download a .glitter.json file you can reopen later with all layers, masks, text, shapes, and uploaded stickers intact.

Export Settings:

Selection: Drag on empty canvas to marquee-select layers; Shift+drag adds to the selection. Use Ctrl/Cmd+A to select every movable layer, Escape to clear a multi-selection, and Alt+drag to duplicate layers while moving them. Alt+click continues to cycle overlapping layers.

Multi-layer Align: Choose Selection or Canvas, then align left/center/right or top/middle/bottom. With three or more layers, distribute them horizontally or vertically. Duplicate and Delete are under Actions.

Base Image – Include/exclude the original image.

Transparency – Preserve transparent areas.

Matte Color – When transparency is disabled, it will replace transparent areas with this color.

Quality – Lower values = better quality but slower (1 is best).

Frame Delay – Animation speed (110ms is classic).

Max Frames – Limit animation length.

Dithering – Algorithm for color reduction.

  • GIFs can contain up to 256 colors from the 24-bit RGB color space.
  • Dithering adds controlled noise or patterns to simulate more colors.
  • Dithering is not available when exporting base images with transparency.

Etcetera

Tips

Try different threshold values to fine-tune your glitter fill selections.

Enable Show Only Active Layer to focus on one layer at a time.

Use Apply Globally for consistent settings across all glitter layers.

Adding more layers or animations with differing frame counts will increase render times.

  • The system figures out a frame sequence that lets all layers loop together smoothly, even if they have different numbers of frames—this can result in many frames and increase the file size. In certain cases, it will try to reduce the number of frames per element (sticker, glitter) to make the animation loop without having to have hundreds of frames.
  • On some devices, rendering large or intricate designs may impact performance.

How to Use a GIF in an Instagram Story

Save the GIF to your device.

Open the location where the GIF is saved, then copy it to your clipboard.

Open Instagram and start a new Story.

Tap the Aa (Text) icon at the top of the screen.

Tap within the text field, then paste the GIF from your clipboard.

Resize, rotate, and position the GIF like you would with any text or sticker.

THIS STEP IS IMPORTANT: Due to a bug with Instagram Stories, you MUST add a GIF from the GIPHY search built into Instagram or else the story will not animate, even though it will animate in the preview before posting.

  • Tap the sticker icon (smiley face square).
  • Select GIF, then add any GIF—it doesn't matter which one. Unless you want it there, then choose what you want.
  • Layer it behind your Glitter Graphic or place it outside the bounds of the story.

Post the damn story as normal.
