Getting Started

Load Your Image

Drag an image onto the dropzone — or click it to browse — to load one (PNG, JPG, or GIF).
  • This becomes your Base Image – the foundation of your canvas for glitter effects and stickers.
  • If your image is larger than 1024 × 1024px, it will be resized to fit the constraints.
  • Maximum file size: 10MB.
No image? Click New Blank Canvas to start from an empty canvas instead — choose its width, height, and background color.

Have a saved project? Click Open Project in the welcome screen, or drag a .json project file onto the main dropzone to restore it.

Project preflight – Before replacing the current project, Glitter reports unavailable stickers or fonts, unknown layer types, and files created by a newer editor version. You can review substitutions or skipped content before choosing Open Anyway. When opening a newer file, avoid overwriting the original because unsupported data may be lost when saving.

Edit the canvas background by selecting the protected Base Image layer. In Canvas Properties, choose Image, Transparent, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient, replace the image without disturbing other layers, or use Canvas Size to enter new dimensions and pick an anchor. Your content keeps its size and position while the canvas is cropped or extended around it. A live outline previews new bounds, and the changes can be undone.

Leaving Auto Glitter before creating its layers asks for confirmation. The Design gallery stays in an instructional empty state until you select a swatch in Color Matches; while that picker is open, the chosen glitter updates only that match.

Auto Glitter opens an Auto Glitter panel and previews real animated glitter directly on the canvas. A compact strip above the navigation controls identifies the temporary preview, shows status only while analyzing, updating, or reporting an error, and provides Exit to discard it. Auto Glitter temporarily switches to the Hand tool; Hand, Zoom, mouse-wheel navigation, Space-drag, and touch pan/pinch remain available, while layer selection, undo/redo, and editing tools stay locked until the preview is created or discarded. Switch Original / Flat / Glitter at the fixed top of the panel to compare the source, posterized colors, and final animated fills; the create and cancel actions stay fixed at the bottom while Palette, Color Matches, and Advanced scroll between them. The Colors and Combine Similar controls update the live result without repeating the expensive image scan. Vibrant prioritizes colorful accents and combines more neutral shades, Balanced keeps more light and shadow structure, and Natural stays closer to the photo's color distribution. Use Advanced to change minimum region detail, clean anti-aliased edges and dither noise, or disable matched-glitter hue tuning. Select a match swatch to open the Design gallery; gallery choices update the live preview in place, and Done returns to Auto Glitter. Use a match's eye button to skip that color, or drag one color row onto another to combine them; on touch devices, drag with the row handle. Cancel, Exit, Escape, replacing the Base Image, or leaving the panel discards the live batch. Create N Layers commits it as normal editable Glitter Fill layers while transparent image areas stay transparent and the Base Image remains unchanged, then restores the previous tool. A rerun can replace the latest generated set or keep it and add another; edited generated layers default to the safer keep-and-add choice.

Basics

Tools

Some tools show context-specific options that appear only when certain elements or layers are selected.

Select Tool (V) – Pick layers and move stickers, text, and shapes. Turn off Auto-Select in the Select options when canvas drags should keep moving the layer already selected in the Layers panel. Nudge the selected layer with the arrow keys (hold Shift for 10px steps).

When a sticker, text, or shape layer is selected, alignment buttons appear:

  • Center layer horizontally
  • Center layer vertically
  • On the canvas, hold Shift and click to add or remove a movable layer.
  • In the Layers panel, Shift-click selects the full range between two layers; CtrlClick/CmdClick toggles any layer, including Glitter Fill, locked, and Base Image layers.
  • Locked layers can be selected and inspected. A multi-selection can only move, align, resize, or rotate when every selected layer is an unlocked Sticker, Text, or Shape. Layer actions remain available when every selected layer permits that action.
  • Hold Alt and click to cycle through overlapping layers under the pointer.

Hand Tool (H) – Click and drag to pan around the canvas. Hold Space to use the Hand tool temporarily, then release it to return to the previous tool.

  • On touch devices, use one or two fingers to pan from any tool; the separate Hand tool is hidden because it is redundant.

Context toolbar alignment options:

  • Center canvas horizontally
  • Center canvas vertically

Color Fill (I) – With a glitter layer or base image selected, click on the canvas to create a color selection based on the base image.

Mask Brush (B) – With a glitter or text layer selected, paint or erase glitter directly on the canvas.

  • See Mask Brush under Glitter for painting modes and brush settings.

Text (T) – Click the canvas to place Point text, or drag to define a Box that wraps text inside a resizable frame.

  • See Text for typography, layout, and fill/border/shadow options.

Shape (U) – Drag on the canvas to draw a shape at that size (Shift = square), or click for a default size.

  • See Shapes for shape choices and fill/border/shadow options.

Zoom Tool (Z) – To zoom in, click the canvas.

  • On touch devices, use two-finger pinch to zoom.
  • On trackpads, hold Ctrl and use the wheel gesture to zoom smoothly.

Context toolbar zoom options:

  • Double-click the status-bar zoom percentage to reset zoom to 100%
  • Fit – Zoom to fit the entire image on screen
  • Fill – Zoom to fill screen with image

Working with Layers

The Layer Panel lets you manage all layers.

Click + to add a new Glitter Fill, Sticker, Text, or Shape layer.

Drag layers up/down to reorder them (top layers appear in front).

  • On touch devices, use the handles to drag layers.

Sticker, text, and shape layers have transform handles: drag inside the box to move, use corner handles to resize or scale, and use the top handle to rotate. Side handles scale stickers and shapes along one axis. For text, Point mode handles scale the text while Box mode handles resize the text box without changing the type size.

  • Hold Shift while rotating — with the handle or the Rotation slider — to snap to 15° increments.
  • Hold Shift while dragging to axis-lock movement. The Transform card also gives exact X/Y, W/H, rotation, opacity, alignment, flip, and reset controls. Arrow keys nudge the selected layer (Shift = 10px).
  • When Lock Aspect is enabled, corner handles and the circular middle edge handles preserve the layer's current aspect ratio.
  • The X/Y/W/H fields support keyboard stepping too: use ↑ or ↓ for 1px changes, or hold Shift for 10px steps.
  • When multiple movable layers are selected, a shared bounding box appears. Drag inside it to move the group, or use the corner handles to scale the group uniformly.
  • On touch, taps still select the topmost layer. To add or remove layers from the selection, use the layer list, then move or pinch the shared group box on the canvas.

Click the eye icon to show/hide layers.

Click the lock icon to prevent canvas transforms and property changes while keeping the layer available for selection and inspection. The Base layer remains editable because its lock means fixed to the canvas rather than read-only.

Click the duplicate icon to copy a layer with all its settings.

Click the trash icon to delete a layer.

Selection & Alignment

Selection: Drag on empty canvas to marquee-select layers; hold Shift while dragging to add to the selection. On the canvas, Shift-click toggles a layer under the pointer. In the Layers panel, Shift-click selects the entire range and CtrlClick/CmdClick toggles any layer, including locked, Base Image, and Glitter Fill layers. A selection containing one of those protected or non-transformable layers stays selectable but cannot move as a group. Use CtrlA/CmdA to select every movable layer, Escape to clear a multi-selection, and hold Alt while dragging to duplicate layers. Hold Alt while clicking to cycle overlapping layers.

Editing shortcuts: Double-click a text layer to focus its text field, or double-click a layer name to rename it. Use CtrlD/CmdD to duplicate the selection. Hold Space to pan temporarily. Hold Alt while resizing to scale from the center. Escape closes active pickers or cancels an active transform before clearing selection. Use CtrlY/CmdY or CtrlShiftZ/CmdShiftZ to redo.

Smart Guides & Snapping – While moving a sticker, text, shape, or multi-layer selection, the editor snaps its edges and center to the canvas and to visible layers. Pink guide lines appear at the active alignment.

  • Snapping is on by default. Use Snapping below the preview to turn it on or off.
  • Hold Ctrl during a move to temporarily disable snapping without changing the toggle.
  • Smart Guides currently assist movement only; resizing and rotation remain freeform, apart from Shift rotation snapping.

Sticker effects: Use Change in the Asset card to choose another sticker. Sticker Properties includes an optional Shadow paint slot with the same controls as the text and shape slots — glitter (with its own swatch and Advanced Hue/Saturation/Brightness), solid color, or gradient sources. Under Actions, use Fit canvas to scale and center a sticker so it fits fully inside the canvas, or Fill canvas to cover the canvas completely.

Multi-layer Align: Choose Selection or Canvas, then align left/center/right or top/middle/bottom. With three or more layers, distribute them horizontally or vertically. Duplicate and Delete are under Actions.

Canvas View Options

Control what you see on the canvas.

Toggle Bounds – Show/hide bounding boxes around stickers.

Toggle Transparency – Show/hide the checkerboard pattern behind transparent areas.

Show Only Selected Layer – Temporarily isolate the selected layer on the canvas. Click again to show every visible layer. This changes only the canvas view, not the export or layer visibility settings.

Glitter

Create a Glitter Layer

In the Layer Panel, click + and choose Glitter Fill.

  • This creates a new layer to add glitter effects.

Choose Your Glitter

Browse the glitter gallery and click any glitter to apply it to your selected colors.

  • Use search and filters to find specific styles, colors, or categories.

Select Colors

Use the Color Fill Tool (I) to click any color in your base image.

  • This tells the glitter layer which parts to fill.
  • A glitter layer will be empty until a selection is made.
  • When a selection is made, the fill will be outlined in blue to better visualize the area (desktop only, it's too resource intensive on mobile). This outline is only visible in the preview.

Selection Options

Multi-Select – Make multiple selections on the same layer.

Threshold – Sets the tolerance for color matching when selecting areas to fill with glitter.

  • Lower values – Selects colors very close to the one clicked for precise selections.
  • Higher values – Selects a broader range of similar colors across the entire image.

Feather – Softens the edges of your selection for smoother blending.

Contiguous – Only selects connected areas of the same color, preventing scattered pixel selection.

Invert – Select everything except the color clicked.

Adjust Glitter Properties

In the Glitter Properties panel, customize your glitter layer:

Use Change in the Fill asset card to open the glitter gallery for the selected layer. The picker strip identifies the destination; choose glitter, then press Done to return to Glitter Properties.

Opacity – Controls transparency (100% = fully visible, 0% = invisible).

Advanced – Expand for Scale (glitter pattern size) plus Hue, Saturation, and Brightness to recolor any glitter swatch without swapping it. Works on fill layers and on the text Fill, Border, and Shadow slots, and matches in the exported GIF.

Texture Scale, Opacity, Threshold, and Feather are adjusted independently for each glitter fill layer.

Mask Brush

Beyond color selections, use the Mask Brush to manually paint or erase exactly where glitter appears — available for glitter fill and text layers.

Click the Mask Brush in the toolbar, or press B, to start painting.

Paint / Erase – Use the Eraser tool in the toolbar (press E, or X to swap between Brush and Eraser) to remove glitter with each stroke instead of adding it — including areas added by color selection. On a pen tablet, flipping to the pen's eraser end erases automatically for that stroke.

Shape, Size, Softness, Flow, Spacing, and Smoothing – Pick the brush tip (round, square, calligraphy, star, or heart), then set its diameter, edge feathering, how much glitter builds up per stroke, how far apart the stamps sit along the stroke, and how much shaky strokes are stabilized. The Brush and Eraser keep their own independent settings and are remembered between sessions.

  • Use [ and ] to change brush size, or hold Shift for larger jumps.

Straight lines – Hold Shift while dragging to constrain the current stroke to a straight line (0°, 45°, or 90°).

Brush Settings and Eraser Settings organize controls into collapsible Brush Tip, Stroke, and Actions groups. Pressure Sensitivity sits with the Stroke dynamics it modifies. Actions can reset the active tool, copy the other tool's current settings, or Clear Paint while keeping color selections intact.

Invert – Flip the mask so glitter covers everything except what's been selected and painted.

Clear Paint – Remove all painted strokes while keeping color selections intact.

Toggle Mask Overlay in the preview controls to see painted areas highlighted while you work.

Stickers

Add Stickers

Create a Sticker layer to add decorative images on top of glitter.

Click + in the Layer Panel and choose Sticker.
  • The sticker layer will be empty until a sticker is chosen.
Browse the sticker gallery and click one to add it.
Use the Select Tool (V) to drag and move the sticker.

Sticker Properties

Opacity – Control transparency.

Rotation – Spin the sticker (–180° to 180°).

Scale X & Y – Resize width and height independently.

Proportional Scale – When enabled, X and Y scale together to maintain aspect ratio. Disable to stretch or squash the sticker.

Flip Horizontally/Vertically – Mirror the sticker.

Text

Create a Text Layer

Click + in the Layer Panel and choose Text, or press T and click (or drag) on the canvas.
  • Clicking places Point text that hugs the glyphs; dragging defines a Box that wraps text inside a resizable frame.
Browse the glitter gallery and click any glitter to fill the text with it, just like a glitter layer.

Typography & Layout

In the Text Properties panel, edit the copy, choose a non-destructive letter case, and control how it is laid out. Double-click or double-tap text to open and select its copy.

Text – Type your copy directly into the text field.

Font – Choose from the built-in font picker.

Font Size and Letter Spacing – Adjust glyph size and the gap between characters.

Point / Box – Point text hugs the copy and scales with the corner handles. Box text wraps inside a frame you can resize with the edge handles.

Line Height – Adjust the spacing between lines when text wraps inside a Box. Not shown for Point text, which is always a single line.

Actions – Use Fit to Text to resize a Box text frame to its current content. It is only available while the layer is in Box mode.

Vertical and Horizontal alignment – Position text within a Box frame (top/middle/bottom) and align lines (left/center/right).

Fill, Border & Shadow

Each part of the text keeps its own source — glitter or solid color.

Fill – Choose None, Glitter, Solid, or Gradient. Texture Scale appears for glitter; Opacity applies to glitter, solid, and gradient sources. Gradients support linear/radial modes, angle with reset, a Blend setting (Linear, Smooth, or Constant hard bands), and color stops with percentage position and opacity. Drag a handle on the gradient preview strip to move a stop, or click the strip to add one.

Border – Enable an outline around the text and set its width. It defaults to glitter — click the border swatch to choose which one, or switch it to Solid Color for a flat color instead.

Shadow – Enable a drop shadow and set its X/Y offset. Like the border, it can use its own glitter or a solid color.

Only one swatch is "active" for glitter picking at a time — click a swatch (Fill, Border, or Shadow) to send the next glitter you pick in the gallery to that part of the text.

Shapes

Draw a Shape

Add clean vector shapes — circle, square, triangle, diamond, pentagon, hexagon, star, or heart — filled with glitter or solid color.

Click the Shape tool in the toolbar, or press U. Drag on the canvas to draw the shape at that size (hold Shift to keep it square or circular), or just click for a default-size shape.

Add shapes from the Shapes area of the Design gallery. If a shape layer is already selected, choosing a gallery shape replaces that layer's shape; otherwise it creates a new shape layer. Shape Properties controls the selected shape's fill, transform, border, and shadow. Resize, rotate, and move it with the transform handles — shapes re-render crisply at their new size.

Fill, Border & Shadow

Each shape has a Fill plus optional Border and Shadow, and every one can be glitter or a solid color.

Fill – Choose None, Glitter, or Solid. Set the fill to None with a Border on to get an outline shape. (The same None option on text makes outlined text.)

Border & Shadow – Enable each and pick its own glitter or solid color, width/offset, and opacity. Borders follow the shape's true outline, so even sharp shapes like the star stay smooth. Shape borders can also switch to Dotted Border mode with adjustable dot spacing. Open Advanced on any slot to recolor its glitter (Hue / Saturation / Brightness).

Export

Exporting

When ready to save, export your design.

Use the Project section in the Canvas panel to name your project. That name is reused for exports and Save Project; leave it blank to use the default file name.

Open Export Settings from the toolbar or export flow to customize output options.

Click Export to generate an animated GIF. MP4 is also available when the browser supports video encoding.

Click Save Project or press CtrlS/CmdS to download a .glitter.json file you can reopen later with all layers, masks, text, shapes, and uploaded stickers intact.

Export Settings:

Format – GIF is available on every supported browser. MP4 appears only when the device supports video encoding and uses the matte color because video does not support transparency.

Video Length (MP4) – Choose Exact duration to end at the entered time, even partway through a loop, or Complete loops to end after a whole number of animation loops. The estimated video length updates before export.

MP4 Quality – Choose Compact, Standard, or High video bitrate.

MP4 result: The result screen previews the video and shows its file size, frame count, duration, and dimensions before you save, open, or share it.

Watermark – Add the optional watermark to the exported result.

Transparency – Preserve transparent areas.

Matte Color – When transparency is disabled, it will replace transparent areas with this color.

Color Quality – Preserve subtle color differences more accurately. Higher quality takes longer to export but does not change the image dimensions.

Fallback Frame Time – Used only when an animation source has no usable timing information. Source GIF timing is otherwise preserved.

Frame Control – Frame Limit is a target for animation complexity, not its length. Optimization Goal chooses between fine motion and a smaller file; Smart Frame Reduction combines frames that look the same without changing playback length. Keep Fewer Frames is a manual override, and Motion Detail controls how often faster visual changes are checked.

Dithering – Helps gradients look smoother when GIF's limited color palette blends similar colors together.

  • GIFs can contain up to 256 colors from the 24-bit RGB color space.
  • Dithering adds controlled noise or patterns to simulate more colors.
  • Dithering is disabled when GIF transparency is active so transparent pixels remain reliable.

Etcetera

Tips

Mobile: Use Design for the gallery, Layers for stacking and selection, and Edit for the active layer's properties. Drag the Edit sheet's handle to expand it or swipe it down to close. App Settings is in the header gear button. Clear All is in the Layers drawer action bar.

Welcome screen: Open it at any time from App Settings. The adjacent switch controls whether it also appears on startup.

Resetting settings: App Settings can restore the saved Brush and Eraser controls or expand all collapsed property and tool groups. Reset All Settings includes both, along with export and interface preferences, without changing the current project or user uploads.

Try different threshold values to fine-tune your glitter fill selections.

Enable Show Only Selected Layer below the preview to focus on one layer at a time without changing the export.

Turn off Auto-Select when you want to choose a layer in the Layers panel and keep moving that layer even when the pointer passes over other artwork.

Adding more layers or animations with differing frame counts will increase render times.

  • The system figures out a frame sequence that lets all layers loop together smoothly, even if they have different numbers of frames—this can result in many frames and increase the file size. In certain cases, it will try to reduce the number of frames per element (sticker, glitter) to make the animation loop without having to have hundreds of frames.
  • On some devices, rendering large or intricate designs may impact performance.

Use an Export in an Instagram Story

Video option: If MP4 is available on the device, choose Exact duration and enter the Story length you need, such as 15 seconds, then export and save or share the video. Instagram may recompress uploaded video, which can soften fine glitter texture or small details; use the High quality preset if the result needs more detail.

To use the GIF clipboard method instead:

Save the GIF to your device.

Open the location where the GIF is saved, then copy it to your clipboard.

Open Instagram and start a new Story.

Tap the Aa (Text) icon at the top of the screen.

Tap within the text field, then paste the GIF from your clipboard.

Resize, rotate, and position the GIF like you would with any text or sticker.

THIS STEP IS IMPORTANT: Due to a bug with Instagram Stories, you MUST add a GIF from the GIPHY search built into Instagram or else the story will not animate, even though it will animate in the preview before posting.

  • Tap the sticker icon (smiley face square).
  • Select GIF, then add any GIF—it doesn't matter which one. Unless you want it there, then choose what you want.
  • Layer it behind your Glitter Graphic or place it outside the bounds of the story.

Post the damn story as normal.
